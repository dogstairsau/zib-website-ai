## 3. Google Business Profile timing: source

Google Business Profile Help, "Add a business opening date" —
https://support.google.com/business/answer/9174409

- An opening date can be set up to one year ahead; only month and year are required.
- "After you enter the date and verify your business, your profile shows up on Google 90 days
  prior to your opening date."
- Posts and photos can be used in that 90-day window (announce the opening, show progress).
- After opening, the profile shows a "Recently opened" tag for 90 days.

For a 1 March 2027 opening, the profile shows from about 1 December 2026, which is why the
SEO kick-off sits in December. Google's help page does **not** promise an "Opening soon" label,
so the page doesn't either.

**Verification risk:** new profiles are usually verified by video (exterior, signage, proof of
management). A construction site with no signage may fail. Plan to verify once the signage is
up; if that's after 1 December, the 90-day window shortens.

**Old listings at the address:** check Google Maps for Hotel Fitzroy, Ichi Ni Nana, Upside
Rooftop, Village People, Old Colonial Inn at 125–129 Brunswick St. Make sure each is marked
permanently closed (suggest an edit if we don't control it). Don't rename an old listing into
the new pub: it's a different business, and its reviews aren't about this pub.
