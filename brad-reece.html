Common questions

Questions we actually get asked.

01

What does "Zib partner" actually mean?

Brad operates as a Zib partner. He's not a Zib employee. His P&L is tied to his clients and outcomes. The incentive model is structurally different from any other agency in the Zib network: when your campaigns work, his business grows. When they don't, his suffers directly. That's why accountability isn't a slogan on this page.

02

Will I be working with Brad directly, or a junior?

Brad owns the relationship, the strategic direction and the accountability. A dedicated senior digital manager handles the day-to-day execution across channels, briefed and reviewed by Brad. So you get two senior brains on your account: partner-level commercial thinking plus senior specialist craft. No juniors running things behind the scenes, no handoff to a service rep.

03

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Trades, manufacturers, wholesalers, professional services, commercial B2B, local retail, ecommerce operators. If the business has a commercial outcome tied to being found on Google (phone calls, form fills, quote requests, online sales), the model applies. From sole operators to multi-site businesses, same playbook, different scale.

04

How long does SEO take to show results?

Technical fixes move the needle in weeks. Content and local rankings compound over three to six months. Anyone promising "page one in 30 days" for commercial keywords is either lying or bidding on your brand name. Brad reports weekly so you see the compounding, not just the finish line.

05

Where does Brad work?

Brad's territory is Brisbane: CBD, inner suburbs, North, South, Bayside, Western corridor. Local SEO, Google Business Profile and citation architecture built around how Brisbane buyers actually search. If you're based here but sell nationally or interstate, his team builds the local and national architecture together. Outside Brisbane, the Zib partner for your region handles you directly. Same model, different face.
