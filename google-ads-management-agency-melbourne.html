Google Ads · Melbourne

Most accounts don't have a bidding problem. They have a tracking problem.

When we audit a Melbourne Google Ads account, the money is rarely being lost where the owner thinks. It's lost to conversions that were never tracked properly, to Performance Max quietly absorbing branded traffic it didn't earn, and to search terms nobody has read in months. No amount of daily bid tinkering fixes any of those.

  • Conversion tracking first, always. Before we touch a bid we make sure the account is counting the right things: form submissions, calls, and where your CRM supports it, the enquiries that actually became revenue. Optimising toward a broken conversion just buys you more of the wrong lead, faster.
  • Phone calls counted as conversions. We run dynamic number tracking, so a call from a Google Ads click is attributed to the keyword that produced it. For trades, health, legal and home services in Melbourne, that's often most of the pipeline — and it's usually invisible in the account we inherit.
  • Search terms read by a human, weekly. Broad match plus Smart Bidding will find you traffic. Whether it finds you buyers depends on someone reading the search terms report and building the negative list. That's a standing weekly job here, not a quarterly clean-up.
  • Performance Max, kept honest. PMax works, but left alone it will happily take credit for branded searches you'd have won for free. We separate brand, control asset groups and exclusions, and report on what PMax actually added rather than what it claimed.
  • Bidding matched to your economics. Target CPA and target ROAS only work if the targets reflect real margin and real close rates. We set them from your numbers, not from the account's historical average.
  • Reported in leads, CPA and ROAS. Live dashboards you can open any time, and a monthly review with the strategist who runs the account. Never an impressions report.

This is what that discipline produces in Melbourne accounts: 418 enquiries in under 12 months at under $45 a lead for a Melbourne pool fencing company, converting at 11.17%. 53 to 110 leads a month for a Melbourne car subscription service across a six-year partnership. And 806 bookings in 9 months from a standing start for a Melbourne and Sydney events business now booked by PRADA, Burberry and Garnier.

PPC management · Melbourne

What a PPC agency in Melbourne is actually managing.

Melbourne is a deep market with a long tail of specialists in every category, which means the expensive head terms are usually the worst value in the account. A plumber bidding on plumber Melbourne is buying the same click as forty competitors, most of whom are also bidding it on broad match. The volume sits there; the margin rarely does.

So the work of managing Google Ads here is mostly the work of getting specific. Suburb and service-level structure instead of one catch-all campaign. Ad copy that qualifies before the click rather than maximising it. Landing pages that match the promise the ad made, because a good click landing on a generic homepage is still a wasted click. And enough negative keyword discipline to keep Smart Bidding pointed at buyers rather than researchers.

Seasonality matters more here than most operators plan for. End of financial year pulls B2B and equipment demand forward into June. Spring moves home services, trades and property. Budgets set once in January and left alone will overspend through the flat months and run out exactly when the auction gets valuable. We pace to the calendar your business actually trades on.

On the name: Google Ads and Google AdWords are the same platform. Google retired the AdWords name in 2018, but plenty of Melbourne businesses still search for AdWords management, and plenty of accounts we take over were built under the old structure — legacy campaign types, outdated match-type assumptions, conversion tracking that predates GA4. If that's the account you're sitting on, the migration is usually the single biggest available gain, ahead of anything we'd do with bids.
