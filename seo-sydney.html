The Sydney market, honestly

What winning Sydney search actually costs.

We'll say the quiet part first: Sydney SEO is the most expensive in the country, because SEO pricing is competition pricing. The workload isn't set by us, it's set by whoever currently holds position one — how much content they've published, how much authority they've earned, how long they've compounded. In Sydney that incumbent is usually well-funded and professionally run. Anyone quoting you the same retainer for Sydney head terms as for a regional market either doesn't know the difference or is planning to do regional-market work and let you find out later.

That's not an argument against the market — it's an argument for entering it deliberately. The businesses that get eaten here are the ones that spend eighteen months chasing a citywide trophy term while competitors quietly take the region and service searches that actually convert. A Northern Beaches plumber doesn't need to outrank the CBD; a Parramatta commercial lawyer doesn't need position one on "lawyer Sydney". The metro is twenty markets wearing one name, and the strategy is choosing which of them you're going to own.

The other Sydney reality: it's where national head offices live, which makes it Australia's deepest B2B search market. Procurement teams, comparison searches, long consideration cycles — B2B search demand here rewards depth of content in a way consumer categories don't, and it converts at deal sizes that change the SEO maths entirely. If you sell to businesses, the keyword list that matters is longer and less obvious than the one your competitors are all bidding on, and that's exactly the kind of gap organic search is best at exploiting.

And on where we sit: Zib is headquartered in Melbourne with clients across every state — the national manufacturer, professional services and ecommerce results cited above were all delivered remotely, because rankings are built by the work, not by the office address. What you get is a dedicated senior digital manager who owns your account outright, whichever city they're sitting in. If a Sydney postcode on the invoice matters more to you than the result, we're the wrong agency — and we'd rather you know that now.
