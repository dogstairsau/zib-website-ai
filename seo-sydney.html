How we approach Sydney

SEO services in Sydney. Revenue that follows.

In Sydney, more agencies and bigger budgets compete for your keywords than anywhere else in Australia. So the plan starts narrow, wins the searches it can actually take, and widens as authority builds. Every month is reported in enquiries and revenue.

  • Foundations before content. In a market this crowded, a slow or badly structured site never catches up, so speed, indexing and Core Web Vitals are fixed before anything new is written.
  • The long tail first, head terms later. Service-plus-suburb searches convert well and can be won early. The broad "Sydney" terms come once the site has earned them.
  • Location pages that earn their place. Each Sydney service area gets its own content, not a template with the suburb swapped. That approach took a refrigerated transport client to first place for its location-specific terms.
  • Authority that survives updates. Links from relevant Australian sites and real relationships. In a market this competitive, shortcuts get found out.
  • The map pack, every month. For Sydney service businesses, Google Business Profile often drives more calls than the website, so it gets the same attention.

Ongoing SEO services or a one-off technical project, the same senior team does the work and reports it in leads and revenue.

The Sydney search market

SEO experts for Sydney, the hardest market in the country.

Sydney is the most contested search market in Australia. More agencies, more budget and more established domains competing for the same commercial terms than anywhere else. A strategy that ranks comfortably in a smaller capital will stall here, and businesses routinely underestimate that.

What works is refusing to fight on the broadest terms first. We build visibility from the searches you can actually win, at suburb and service level across the Eastern Suburbs, the Inner West, the North Shore, Parramatta and the Sutherland Shire, then use that authority to move upward into the head terms.

Whether you're looking for search engine optimisation for a new website, SEO experts to take over an existing programme, or a Sydney SEO company that will tell you honestly whether the head terms are realistic for your budget, that's the conversation we'd rather have first.

Inheriting another agency's work

Three suburbs, or all of Sydney?
