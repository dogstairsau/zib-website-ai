Search engine optimisation in Sydney

The hardest SEO market in the country.

Sydney is the most contested search market in Australia. More agencies, more budget and more established domains competing for the same commercial terms than anywhere else. A strategy that ranks comfortably in a smaller capital will stall here, and businesses routinely underestimate that.

What works is refusing to fight on the broadest terms first. We build visibility from the searches you can actually win, at suburb and service level across the Eastern Suburbs, the Inner West, the North Shore, Parramatta and the Sutherland Shire, then use that authority to move upward into the head terms.

Whether you're looking for search engine optimisation for a new website, SEO specialists to take over an existing programme, or a Sydney SEO company that will tell you honestly whether the head terms are realistic for your budget, that's the conversation we'd rather have first.

Common questions

What Sydney businesses actually ask us.

Sydney is the most competitive SEO market in Australia, and pricing reflects that. Meaningful retainers for a competitive Sydney category generally start in the low four figures a month and rise with the ambition of the keyword set. Below that there isn't enough senior time and content production to move a market this contested. We'd rather scope to a narrower set of suburbs or services and win them than spread a small budget thinly across greater Sydney. If you want to test the maths first, our ROI calculator models SEO against your margins and lead values.

Ask three questions. Who specifically will work on the account day to day, rather than who is in the pitch? What happens to the site, the content and the links if you leave? And can they show you their own rankings? An SEO company that can't rank itself in its own city is asking you to take a lot on faith. We rank on our own commercial terms, which is the only demonstration that doesn't rely on a case study you can't verify.

Technical fixes on an established domain can move positions within weeks. Competitive Sydney head terms typically take 9 to 12 months, longer than most other Australian capitals because the incumbents are stronger. Suburb-level and long-tail terms usually produce enquiries far sooner, which is why we sequence them first and use that momentum to fund the longer fight.
