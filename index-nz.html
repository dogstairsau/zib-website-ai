Questions

The things we're asked most often

How is Zib different from traditional digital agencies?
Traditional agencies sell activity — reports, impressions, hours. We sell the commercial outcome those things are supposed to produce. Every engagement starts with your number (leads, sales, revenue), and every tactic ladders back to it. Our AI operating stack handles the heavy lifting so our senior strategists spend their time on the commercial conversation, not manual reporting.
Who do you work with?
From local trades doing seven figures to ASX-listed brands running national campaigns. The common thread: owners and marketing leads who want a real commercial conversation, not a channel pitch. Typically $5K–$15K monthly media spend, 4–6 hours a week on your side.
How do I know your approach will work for me?
You don't, until we look at your numbers together. Drop your URL into the audit above — it takes 30 seconds and gives you a real Lighthouse read plus a senior strategist's interpretation. From there, if the commercial conversation makes sense, we go further. No capability decks, no pitch theatre.
Should I hire internal or agency?
Depends on scale. Sub-$50K/year marketing budgets rarely justify a senior hire. North of that, the decision is about depth vs breadth — a single hire can go deep on one channel, but an agency with an AI operating stack delivers senior thinking across every channel at lower total cost. We do both: run everything for businesses without internal teams, or plug into existing teams as the strategic + AI layer.
How much does Zib cost?
Service fees scale with scope, typically $3K–$12K a month plus media spend. Every engagement has a 90-day cancellation clause — if results stall, we pivot, or step back. We don't lock clients into year-long retainers to protect revenue.
How do you report?
Weekly. Plain English. Numbers that tie back to your commercial metric. No 40-page PDFs. Your dashboard shows revenue, not vanity, and you know exactly what's moving the needle every week.
Do you work with internal marketing teams?
Yes, often. We slot in as the senior strategic + AI operating layer on top of an existing team. Your team keeps their day-to-day; we bring the commercial framing, the AI tooling, and the channel depth. Brief once, scale out everywhere.
