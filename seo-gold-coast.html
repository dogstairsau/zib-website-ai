Search engine optimisation on the Gold Coast

Two economies. Two kinds of search.

The Gold Coast runs two economies side by side, and they search differently. The visitor economy of accommodation, tours, hospitality and events is seasonal, and its customers are searching from Sydney, Melbourne or overseas, so the "Gold Coast" head terms matter and proximity to the searcher does not. The resident economy of trades, home services, health and professional services is local, year-round and suburb-qualified: Southport, Burleigh, Robina, Coomera, Helensvale.

Most Gold Coast SEO goes wrong by applying one plan to both. A tradie chasing "Gold Coast" head terms is competing with directories and booking platforms for searches that were never going to become jobs. A tour operator building suburb pages is optimising for people who already live here.

We work out which economy you sell into before writing a page, build the suburb and service coverage the map pack rewards where it applies, and time the seasonal work so it has ranked by the time the season arrives, not after it.

SEO for a new website

Paid alongside organic
