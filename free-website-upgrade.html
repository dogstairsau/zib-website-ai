A few quick answers.

Is this a full website redesign?

No. The free upgrade includes one dedicated landing page aligned with your marketing goals. Your team will agree the scope with you before building.

Who can apply?

The offer is available for a limited time to selected clients engaged with Zib Digital’s marketing services. Submit your details and our team will confirm your eligibility.

What does “free” cover?

The dedicated landing-page build described above is free for approved clients. We’ll confirm the agreed inclusions and any requirements relating to your existing website or services before work begins.

What happens after I apply?

Our team will get in touch to discuss your website, confirm eligibility and understand what you want to improve. Once approved, we’ll agree the scope and prepare your page for launch.

Will it guarantee more enquiries?

The page is designed to encourage enquiries. Results also depend on your offer, traffic, market and follow-up, so we don’t guarantee a specific increase.
