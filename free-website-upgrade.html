Offer terms & FAQs.

* Free means no additional landing-page build cost within the agreed scope. Marketing service fees and advertising spend remain payable separately. The offer is subject to eligibility, written approval and the terms below.

Who is eligible, and how is the offer accepted?

This limited-time offer is available to selected clients engaged with Zib Digital’s marketing services. The offer is intended for businesses already running paid ads, with an ongoing advertising budget, seeking an agency partner and planning growth over the next six months. It is not suitable if you have no advertising budget or do not want to engage an agency. Zib selects suitable clients based on marketing requirements, technical suitability and delivery capacity. Eligibility must be confirmed by Zib in writing. Once accepted as part of an agreed proposal, the complimentary landing page terms govern delivery.

What does the complimentary upgrade include?

The design, build and launch of one dedicated landing page at no additional build cost. The applicable marketing services, page scope, included functionality and pre-launch revisions will be specified in the proposal or otherwise agreed in writing before work starts. A full website redesign, additional pages, custom functionality, integrations and additional content production are excluded unless expressly included. Marketing service fees and advertising spend remain payable separately.

Is hosting included, and will the page always be available?

Standard hosting and operation are provided at no additional charge while you continue the marketing services identified in your proposal, subject to the agreement’s payment, suspension and termination provisions. Reasonable maintenance, technical faults or third-party interruptions may affect availability. Zib will take reasonable steps to minimise disruption and restore availability.

What happens if my marketing services end?

Your entitlement to use the landing page ends when the applicable marketing services end. Zib may take it offline after giving written notice of the scheduled removal date. Continued hosting, transfer or purchase must be separately agreed in writing. There is no retrospective charge for the complimentary build. Removing this landing page does not transfer ownership of, or authorise deletion of, your existing website, domain or separately owned materials.

Where will the content and images come from?

We’ll use images and existing content from your current website, adapted within the agreed scope. You authorise this use and confirm you have the necessary rights or permissions for materials you supply or approve for reuse. Additional copywriting, photography, stock imagery, video, graphics or other content outside the agreed scope will be scoped and quoted separately. We’ll obtain your written approval of the scope and charges before proceeding.

What do I need to provide, and when will the page launch?

You’ll need to provide reasonably required access, materials and feedback, and review the page before launch. You are responsible for confirming the accuracy of your business information, offers, pricing and claims. Zib will obtain your written approval before publishing. Delivery dates depend on timely receipt of the required materials and approvals.

Can I request changes after launch?

Yes. Client-requested changes to the design, content, layout, functionality or integrations will be scoped and quoted separately after launch. Work proceeds only after you approve the scope and charges in writing. This does not apply to correcting Zib’s errors, rectifying failures to meet the agreed scope or fulfilling obligations that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Does the landing page guarantee results?

The page is designed to support your marketing objectives. Zib does not guarantee a particular number of enquiries, conversion rate, search ranking, revenue or sales outcome.

How do these terms relate to my existing agreement?

These terms supplement your marketing services agreement and apply specifically to the complimentary landing page offer. Any inconsistency relating to this offer should be expressly resolved in the signed proposal. Nothing in these terms excludes, restricts or modifies any right, guarantee or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded, restricted or modified.
