Commercial Door & Shutter Manufacturer · Australia

The challenge

An Australian commercial door and shutter manufacturer needed to grow online visibility and lead generation through a combined SEO and Google Ads strategy.

The strategy

A dual-channel approach pairing SEO with Google Ads. Organic search built consistent, compounding traffic and the majority of enquiries. Paid search captured high-intent commercial buyers searching for specific products.

The results · 18 months
  • 41,328 sessions, organic search drove 64% of all traffic (26,529 sessions)
  • 1,867 conversions across phone calls, chatbot enquiries and email clicks
  • Google drove 76% of all conversions
  • SEO: 824,000+ search impressions, 6,885 organic clicks, 696 keyword position improvements
  • 1st for "commercial concertina doors", 3rd for "polycarbonate roller shutters", 5th for "commercial door repair"
  • 1,166 conversions from organic search alone
  • Google Ads: $18,859 spend, 483 conversions at $39.02 per conversion, 9.33% conversion rate
  • GBP impressions up 124%, 447 call clicks
The takeaway

A dual-channel lead engine delivered nearly 1,900 qualified enquiries over 18 months. Organic did the heavy lifting on volume. Google Ads delivered efficient cost-per-lead on commercial terms.
