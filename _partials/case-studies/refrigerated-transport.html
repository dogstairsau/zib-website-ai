Refrigerated Transport & Cold Storage · Australia

The challenge

An Australian refrigerated transport and cold storage provider needed to build their organic search presence and drive qualified enquiries through SEO, without paid search spend.

The strategy

A focused SEO strategy built around location-specific landing pages across the client's service areas (Sydney, Canberra, Newcastle, Orange, Nowra) to capture high-intent local search traffic and convert it into phone calls and quote requests.

The results · 6 months
  • 11,350 sessions, organic search drove 67% of all traffic (7,593 sessions)
  • 637 conversions: 337 phone call clicks, 249 contact forms, 51 quote requests
  • 80,639 search impressions, 4,269 clicks, 361 keyword position improvements
  • Average ranking position 8th across tracked keywords
  • 1st for location-specific terms, 2nd for "refrigerated transport interstate", 3rd for "refrigerated transport" (590/mo)
  • GBP interactions up 359%, desktop search impressions up 52%, 4.64 star review rating
The takeaway

In six months, SEO delivered 637 qualified enquiries, averaging over 100 leads per month, without any paid search spend. Page-one rankings across multiple service locations. Organic search now accounts for two-thirds of all traffic.
