Online Trampoline & Outdoor Recreation Retailer · Australia

The challenge

The client needed to reduce reliance on paid channels by building a strong organic search presence that could drive product visibility and online sales in one of Australia's most competitive ecommerce categories.

The strategy

A comprehensive SEO program: technical optimisation (301 redirects, sitemaps, Core Web Vitals, schema markup), on-page improvements (meta tags, page copy, image compression, internal linking) and ongoing content and backlink acquisition.

The results · 15 months
  • $638,600 in revenue attributed directly to organic search
  • 77,134 organic sessions — largest non-paid traffic source
  • 65,809 organic clicks (up 144%) from 3.87M impressions (up 198%)
  • 32 keyword position improvements, average ranking 12.4
  • 1st position for "trampolines" (60,500/mo), "in ground trampoline", "trampoline net", "trampoline springs"
  • 2nd position for the core term "trampoline" (60,500/mo)
  • GBP impressions up 159%, 1,023 website clicks, 199 call clicks
The takeaway

SEO now accounts for over $638,000 in direct revenue and is the site's most valuable non-paid channel. An organic foundation that drives consistent sales without ongoing ad spend.
