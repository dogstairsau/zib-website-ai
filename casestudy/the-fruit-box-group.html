The Fruit Box Group Digital Marketing Case Study

Learn how The Fruit Box Group increased organic traffic by 250% and boosted leads by 300% with SEO and paid campaigns from our digital marketing agency.

Supplying consumables like fresh fruit, milk and other tea room supplies to offices, The Fruit Box Group began as a start-up in Melbourne. From humble beginnings in 2000, they have grown to become a national distribution business supplying to corporates and organisations.

In the business’ infancy, traditional marketing added value, but as the business expanded, they needed a sophisticated digital marketing strategy to support sustained growth.

Engage with a digital agency with the capability to support strong business growth.

Maintain position as market leader while expanding into new markets.

The Fruit Box Group engaged Zib to provide digital marketing expertise across a range of disciplines and rely on Zib to provide strategic advice and then execute the plan.

With Zib, The Fruit Box Group have experienced a 300% increase in leads, an increase of 250% in organic traffic and a 95% decrease in the average cost per conversion (CPC) on social media.

With previous agencies, the marketing team at The Fruit Box Group were only supplied with a performance report at the end of the month, which made real-time decision making tough.

The strength of Zib and The Fruit Box Group’s professional relationship is based on transparent communication to help the team make informed strategic decisions. This is achieved through a dashboard providing real-time data as well as regular strategy meetings.

Zib has supported The Fruit Box Group to expand into new markets through innovative strategies that have generated more leads and revenue.
