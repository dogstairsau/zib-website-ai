The business is constantly looking at innovative ways to grow and while they do most of their marketing in-house, they chose to outsource their SEO and Google Adwords with a focus on increasing brand performance and engaging audiences more effectively online.

With the consumer landscape continually shifting and evolving, it is essential for their digital marketing strategy to be frequently revisited and optimised.

Liberty Financial felt confident that Zib could drive and support sustained growth through the creation and execution of a long-term plan with continual optimisation and capitalisation on new growth opportunities.

Engage a digital agency as an extension of in-house marketing team who can support digital transformation, company growth and engage audiences effectively online.

In the past year with Zib, Liberty Financial’s organic website traffic has doubled, with an increase of 80% in paid leads and a 70% reduction on average cost per conversion. Liberty Financial now ranks in the top 3 search results on Google for all of their major keywords.

Through total transparency and real-time dynamic results facilitating business expansion, Liberty Financial now see Zib as an extension of their marketing team and appreciate the fact they have a direct line of contact with Zib team members, allowing them to have constructive meetings and quick turnaround times on new strategies.
