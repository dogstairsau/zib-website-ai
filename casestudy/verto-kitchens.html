Verto Kitchens Digital Marketing Case Study

Learn how Verto Kitchens reached the top ranking for 14 key search terms and doubled organic traffic in six months.

Zib Digital has worked with Verto Kitchens for several years. When the time came to push for another phase of growth, we expanded the partnership by adding two more channels — pay-per-click and paid social — alongside a crucial restructure of the website.

That combination unlocked a remarkable six to eight months of growth and helped Verto establish clear dominance over their competitors.

Verto achieved the No.1 ranking for 14 key search terms and saw organic traffic double over the period. Google Ads delivered more than 150 leads at a conversion rate of 60%, while paid social generated a further 27 leads in a little over two months.
