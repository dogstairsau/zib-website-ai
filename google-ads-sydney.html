Paid search in Sydney

The most expensive clicks in the country.

In most commercial categories, Sydney clicks are the most expensive in Australia. More advertisers, deeper budgets and national brands defending their patch mean the auction is crowded before you arrive. A structure that returns comfortably in Adelaide can lose money here on the same keywords.

That changes the job. The margin for error in a Sydney account is smaller, so search term hygiene, match type discipline and conversion data quality are not housekeeping, they are the strategy. Most inherited Sydney accounts are paying for searches that were never going to convert and being told the account is "performing".

The other Sydney-specific decision is geography. Greater Sydney is large enough that a plumber in the Sutherland Shire and a showroom in Alexandria should not be bidding on the same map. We build location targeting around where the business can actually take the job, and bid up or down by area based on what it produces.

Account takeover vs new build

An inherited Sydney account carries conversion history worth keeping and structure usually worth replacing. We audit before we touch anything. A new account starts with tracking, then a tight Search build, and earns the budget for more.

Search vs PMax vs Demand Gen

Search captures intent. Performance Max finds volume but will cannibalise brand traffic without exclusions. Demand Gen creates interest that Search later captures. Each is measured against the others, not reported in isolation.

Call tracking and lead attribution

Sydney service businesses close on the phone. Dynamic call tracking, enhanced conversions and offline import of closed jobs mean the account learns from settlements and sales, not from form fills.
