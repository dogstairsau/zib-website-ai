Signs your stormwater is failing

Don't wait for the next big storm

Water pooling after rain

Ponding against the house or in the yard usually means the drain can't carry the flow away.

Gutters or downpipes overflowing

If the gutters are clear, the blockage is often underground.

Damp walls or subfloor

Leaking stormwater pipes can undermine footings and cause rising damp.

Sinking paths or soft ground

A broken pipe washes soil away beneath paving and lawns.

Gurgling or bad smells

Partial blockages from roots or silt build up before they fail completely.

Renovating or extending

New roof areas and footprints often need drains upsized or relocated.

Common questions

Before you call

How much does a stormwater repair cost?

It depends on depth, access, pipe length and what's above it (lawn, concrete, paving). That's why we inspect first and give a fixed written price covering excavation, pipework and reinstatement before any work starts.

Do I need a CCTV inspection?

If the cause isn't obvious, yes. A camera shows exactly where and why the drain has failed, so you pay to fix the actual problem rather than dig in the wrong place.

Will you damage my garden or driveway?

We plan the excavation route to minimise disruption and reinstate the surface when we finish. Anything that can't be matched like-for-like is discussed and priced upfront.

Can you relocate drains for our renovation?

Yes. We regularly relocate and upsize stormwater and sewer drains for extensions and new builds, and coordinate with your builder or architect.

Is the work certified?

All work is completed by licensed plumbers (VBA licence 116917) to Victorian standards, with compliance certificates issued where required.
