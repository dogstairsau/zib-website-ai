Paid search in Perth

A long, thin city. Bid like it.

Perth stretches a long way north to south, and a plumber in Joondalup and a plumber in Rockingham are not competing for the same customers. Most inherited Perth accounts target "Perth" as one location and let the auction decide where the money goes. We build location targeting around where you can actually take the job, north or south of the river, and bid up or down by area on what each one produces.

The second Perth decision is the rest of the state. A Perth supplier is often the supplier for the Pilbara, the Goldfields and the South West, and regional buyers search with real intent and less competition. If you can supply them, they get their own campaigns with their own budgets rather than a share of the metro one.

We have done this here. Our Perth 4x4 upgrades client generated 1,368 leads at $9.96 each over ten months on a modest budget, most of them phone calls. The search term data from that account also tells the Perth SEO team where to build next.

Account takeover vs new build

An inherited Perth account carries conversion history worth keeping and structure usually worth replacing. We audit before we touch anything. A new account starts with tracking, then a tight Search build, and earns the budget for more.

Search vs PMax vs Demand Gen

Search captures intent. Performance Max finds volume but will cannibalise brand traffic without exclusions. Demand Gen creates interest that Search later captures. Each is measured against the others, not reported in isolation.

Call tracking and lead attribution

Perth trades and industrial suppliers close on the phone. Dynamic call tracking, enhanced conversions and offline import of closed jobs mean the account learns from won work, not from form fills.
