In the very competitive tree services industry, it’s their professional approach, friendly customer service and high quality work that sets Pro Cut apart from the rest. In the past, they had engaged other digital agencies and found there wasn’t a lot of transparency around the approach or performance issues. A friend who was happy with the results Zib was achieving for their business referred the Pro Cut team to Zib. Impressed from the initial face to face meeting, Pro Cut started with Zib in 2012 with a brand-new website, which initially brought in 2-5 leads a day.
Fast forward to today, Zib’s SEO and Google Ads strategy is now bringing in 20-30 leads per day. With a multi-faceted digital marketing strategy in place, Pro Cut Tree Services has experienced an increase of over 1000% in users to their website, with over 50% of these site users coming via organic search. Page one SEO rankings for their keywords has also been achieved and Pro Cut Tree Services are now the largest residential tree removal company in Melbourne. They have been able to grow their team from 5 staff to 25 staff, from 1 truck to 5 trucks and are now in the position to expand into the national market.
