Why Good SEO Doesn’t Always Equal Good Leads
Most businesses come to a digital marketing agency in Australia hoping to appear higher on Google. And yes, rankings matter. But getting onto page one doesn’t guarantee that people will trust your brand, navigate your site easily, or take action.
We’ve seen sites that rank well but still underperform because:
The design feels outdated or cluttered
The mobile version is slow or hard to navigate
Contact forms are confusing or too long
Content doesn’t answer the user’s search intent
Calls to action are buried or vague
When SEO efforts focus only on keywords or backlinks, these crucial elements often get overlooked. The result? Lots of traffic, few conversions.
