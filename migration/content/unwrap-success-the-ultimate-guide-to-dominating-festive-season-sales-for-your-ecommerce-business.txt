The festive season isn't just a time for joy and celebration - it's a golden opportunity for your eCommerce business to shine. As consumers embrace the holiday spirit (and loosen their purse strings), seasonal marketing is an essential strategy to embrace. This approach offers a multitude of benefits, including increased sales, heightened brand awareness and lead generation.
To succeed, it’s crucial to plan ahead by identifying key dates and events for your target audience and create a marketing strategy that includes various promotional strategies. As the premier digital marketing agency in Australia, we're experts at embracing seasonal marketing. In this blog, we’re unwrapping the best ways to promote your eCommerce business during the festive season and make your brand the star of the show.
Start early with Christmas promotions
The early bird catches the worm and early festive promotions catch the customer. Kick off your Christmas marketing strategy well in advance. Shoppers often start browsing for gifts as early as October or November. Creating a countdown to Christmas sale or special holiday offers creates anticipation and keeps your brand top of mind.
Leverage the power of social media advertising
With billions of active users, social media platforms are a treasure trove for your eCommerce business. Tailor your social media ads for the festive season using various formats to increase your reach and engagement.
Facebook Carousel Ads: Showcase holiday themed products or tell a festive story through multiple images or videos
Instagram Stories Ads: Engage users with full screen, quick slideshows or sneak peeks of your holiday collection
Pinterest Promoted Pins: Use pins to showcase holiday specials or best sellers in holiday themed settings
LinkedIn Sponsored Content: If you offer corporate gifts, target business professionals on this platform and highlight your products
Harness the potential of Google Ads
Google Ads offers versatile options to reach potential customers at different stages of their buyer's journey. Remember to optimise your ad copy and keywords for the holiday season to maximise your campaign’s effectiveness.
Google Search Ads: Use holiday related keywords to attract shoppers searching for gift ideas and sales
Google Shopping Ads: Showcase products with images, prices and holiday specials
Google Display Ads: Create festive themed ads for brand awareness and retargeting
Google Local Services Ads: Attract local customers with holiday offers in physical stores
Implement a retargeting ad campaign
Don't forget shoppers who showed interest but didn't complete purchases. Gently remind potential customers about your products and entice them back to your site to finish what they started.
Google Ads Remarketing: Display targeted ads to users who have visited your site as they browse other sites on the Google Display Network or search for related products
Facebook Pixel: Track users visiting your site and serve them retargeting ads on Facebook or Instagram
LinkedIn Retargeting: Target B2B users who visited your site or have taken specific actions
Email Retargeting: Send targeted emails, such as abandoned cart reminders, with festive subject lines
Use Google Analytics to understand customer behaviour
Leverage Google Analytics 4 to track, understand and enhance your customers' holiday shopping behaviour. Don’t forget to regularly review your analytics reports to make data driven decisions for a successful holiday season.
Enhanced measurement: Track key interactions on your site to understand which holiday content engages customers the most
Event tracking: Monitor specific actions customers take during the holiday season to identify potential roadblocks and enhance the shopping experience
Conversion tracking: Define crucial actions such as conversions to understand which marketing efforts are most effective
User centric reporting: Track your customers' journey across various devices and platforms
Advanced analysis: Use advanced analysis techniques to gain deeper insights into customer behaviour
Predictive metrics: Identify high value customers to target with personalised Christmas deals and offers
Create holiday themed content for your eCommerce site
By adding festive touches to your website, you can both delight your customers and boost sales. Here’s some of our top ways to create holiday themed content for your site:
Seasonal design: Add holiday graphics, change colour schemes and create a cohesive holiday theme across pages
Festive product images: Update product images with a holiday touch to showcase them as perfect Christmas gifts
Holiday gift guides: Curate gift guides for different themes, price points and recipients
Special offers and deals: Highlight Christmas discounts, free gift wrapping or holiday bundles
Festive blog posts: Write about holiday decorating ideas, share Christmas recipes or provide gift buying tips
Holiday countdown: Create excitement with a Christmas countdown and daily deals
Customer reviews and testimonials: Showcase top rated Christmas gifts based on customer feedback
Promote exclusive holiday deals and discounts
Get creative with your deals – some of our favourites include bundle deals, buy one get one free (BOGO) offers and free gifts with purchase. Promote these exclusive offers across all your marketing channels to drive sales.
As the festive season approaches, remember that strategic seasonal marketing can elevate your eCommerce business to new heights. By planning ahead, utilising social media, Google Ads, retargeting, Google Analytics, creating festive content and offering exclusive deals, you can unwrap success and make the most of this magical time of year. So, get ready to spread Christmas cheer to your customers and boost your business in the process. Happy holidays!
