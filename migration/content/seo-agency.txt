Local vs National SEO: Which is Right for You?
The key difference between local and national SEO lies in their focus. Local SEO targets a specific geographical area, aiming to attract local customers and top search results. National SEO, on the other hand, seeks to improve visibility and attract customers across the country or globally, requiring a broader scope of targeting.
