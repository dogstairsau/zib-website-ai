Data migration and compatibility
Setting up data collection for GA4 can be a complex and time consuming task. To ensure a smooth process, it’s essential to invest time in detailed planning and preparation. Conducting a thorough audit of your existing data and configurations, ensuring data cleanliness and mapping out the desired data structure in GA4 will help you anticipate potential issues so you can address challenges up front. Explore and utilise the new features unique to GA4
While GA4 offers exciting new features, there may be a steep learning curve to get familiar with the new platform. We recommend you take some time to explore GA4's unique features and learn about its enhanced data collection techniques, such as enhanced cross-device tracking, machine learning capabilities and an event-driven data model. The built in custom reporting option in GA4 requires a significant number of hours to learn and master.
Looker Studio (formerly known as Google Data Studio) offers robust reporting features and a range of customisation options. Users have the ability to integrate GA4 data with Looker Studio, empowering them to create tailored reports that align with their specific needs. With Looker Studio's flexibility and customisation features, you can develop comprehensive, visually appealing dashboards that provide detailed insights into your data.
