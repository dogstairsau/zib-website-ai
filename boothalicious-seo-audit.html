Executive summary

The site has gone backwards, and the cause is fixable.

Over the past 12 months organic traffic fell 59%, organic keywords collapsed 92%, and the site now ranks for essentially nothing outside its own brand name.

The cause is not a competitive or algorithmic squeeze. Three key commercial pages were removed or broken, and the site simultaneously accumulated a large volume of spam backlinks. Layered on top is a serious performance problem: the homepage scores 37 / 100 on mobile Lighthouse with a Largest Contentful Paint of 27.7 seconds, driven by an ~8 MB page weight.

The opportunity is real and winnable. The Australian photo booth market is small and the incumbents are not strong. The combined wedding, corporate and 360 / glam booth keyword clusters represent roughly 3,000–4,000 monthly Australian searches at low-to-moderate difficulty.

The three highest-leverage actions
  1. Repair what's brokenRestore the deleted service and location pages, and resolve the 404s that are currently breaking internal links.
  2. Cut the page weightFix Core Web Vitals. Nothing new will rank while mobile takes 27.7 seconds to render its main content.
  3. Build the money pagesDedicated wedding and corporate hubs — and evaluate adding a 360 / glam booth to the fleet.
Scope note

This audit covers organic search and site health. It is a separate discipline to the Google Ads programme, which continues to run and is reported on separately — paid traffic actually grew over this period (7 → 30 monthly visits).

2.4 — Accessibility, best practices & agentic browsing

Gaps, not a rewrite

Agentic browsing fails on a malformed accessibility tree — the same structure AI assistants read.

Full detail — accessibility, best practices, agentic browsing

Accessibility 88 / 85

Insufficient colour contrast — a known constraint of the pink-on-pink palette. <iframe> with no title. Links with no discernible name. Heading order not sequentially descending — also an SEO issue, as it weakens topical signals. 24 audits pass, so these are gaps rather than a rebuild.

Best practices 73

One third-party cookie. Browser console errors. Issues flagged in DevTools. /pricing scores 100 here, so the failures are tied to homepage-specific embeds — most likely the Instagram feed and Google reviews widget, not the Squarespace template.

Agentic browsing 1 / 2

Fails on "Accessibility tree is not well-formed." This is the same structure AI assistants use to read and act on a page — consistent with the zero AI citations in Part 1, and increasingly relevant as agents mediate discovery and booking.

2.5 — Fix priority

In this order

  1. Resize hero and gallery imageryWebP / AVIF, responsive sizes. Should strip 3–4 MB and move LCP from 27.7 s into single digits on its own.
  2. Reduce JavaScriptAudit third-party embeds — the Instagram feed is expensive and sits below the fold. Defer and lazy-load non-critical scripts; remove legacy polyfills.
  3. Reduce animation loadCut the animated element count, or move animations to transform / opacity so they run on the compositor.
  4. Quick winsfont-display: swap, cache headers, explicit image width / height, colour contrast, iframe titles, link names, heading order.

3.3 — Recommended architecture

The structure that actually ranks

Modelled on In The Booth's working structure — their Adelaide location page alone earns 528 visits a month; Darwin 279.

/ ├── /events/ │ ├── /events/wedding-photo-booth-hire/ │ ├── /events/corporate-photo-booth-hire/ │ ├── /events/brand-activations/ │ ├── /events/engagement-parties/ │ ├── /events/birthday-parties/ │ └── /events/school-formals/ │ ├── /locations/ │ ├── /locations/photo-booth-hire-melbourne/ │ └── /locations/photo-booth-hire-sydney/ │ ├── /booths/ │ ├── /booths/open-booth/ ← restore │ ├── /booths/enclosed-booth/ ← restore (404) │ ├── /booths/branded-booth/ │ ├── /booths/vintage-booth/ │ └── /booths/360-booth/ ← new, if viable │ ├── /venues/ venue partner pages │ └── /venues/[venue-name]/ │ └── /guides/ wedding planning content

Rules

  • Every wedding and corporate page gets its own enquiry form — not a link back to a generic /booknow
  • One strong national wedding page, not a dozen thin city-wedding pages — Melbourne-specific wedding terms are too thin to support them
  • Fix /locations — it claims 3 locations and lists 2
  • Resolve the /enclosed-booth 404 before anything else
  • Consolidate /home, /info, /poh via canonical or redirect

Venue partner tactic

In The Booth runs /partners/[venue-name] pages for individual reception venues — Bracknell Lodge, Eatons Hill Hotel, Crown Perth, The Star Gold Coast — and ranks for venue-name searches. This intercepts couples researching a specific venue and builds genuine local relevance.

A 15-year Melbourne and Sydney operator almost certainly already has photography from dozens of venues.

Part 5 — Beyond SEO

Two things that aren't rankings.

Google Business Profile

For "photo booth hire melbourne" and "photo booth near me" — 200 searches a month at KD 0 — the local pack sits above organic results. A 4.9 rating across 54 reviews is a strong asset that should be actively worked, not left to accumulate on its own.

Conversion path separation

The site currently sends every visitor down the same "Book Now" path. A bride comparing three suppliers and a marketing manager pricing a product launch have completely different buying processes. Separating those paths will likely lift lead volume more in the short term than any ranking gain.

Methodology & caveats

How this was put together, and what it can't tell you

Data sources. Ahrefs Site Explorer and Keywords Explorer (AU market, subdomains mode, Changes: Last year); Google PageSpeed Insights running Lighthouse 13.4.1; a Screaming Frog crawl; and direct inspection of boothalicious.com.au. Audit window: 6 August 2025 – 6 August 2026, compiled 6 August 2026.

1 — Ahrefs traffic figures are modelled estimates

Not measured sessions. Directional conclusions — the scale and shape of the decline — are reliable; absolute numbers are not. Cross-check against Google Search Console and GA4 to pin down exactly when it happened. If the timeline aligns with the 378 new referring domains, or with a site migration or Squarespace template change, that materially changes the diagnosis.

2 — Confirm the link history before disavowing

The backlink pattern is consistent with purchased or automated links, but equally consistent with passive scraper-directory accumulation, which is harmless and best ignored. Ask directly whether any link-building service was engaged in the past 18 months. Do not file a disavow on the basis of this report alone.

3 — Lighthouse figures are lab data

CrUX field data is unavailable because traffic is below Google's reporting threshold, so real-world experience may differ. The magnitude of the image-weight problem is unambiguous regardless.

4 — The content gap registered 2 of 3 competitors

rewindbooths.com.au was dropped by the tool; the 302-keyword figure reflects inthebooth.com.au and adeptphotobooths.com.au only. The true gap is likely larger.
