Facebook & Instagram advertising

Creative is the new targeting.

Facebook and Instagram advertising has changed shape completely since the iOS privacy changes. Targeting does less work than it did, the algorithm does more, and creative volume has become the single biggest lever on performance. Most underperforming Melbourne accounts we inherit are still built around the old model: narrow audiences, a handful of ads, and a media buyer optimising audiences that Meta stopped needing help with years ago.

We run Facebook and Instagram as one creative testing system: broad targeting, a steady supply of new creative, and ruthless removal of what doesn't earn its impressions. The account structure is deliberately simple, because complexity now mostly starves the algorithm of the data it needs.

Whether you're after a Facebook ads agency, Meta advertising management or a Melbourne team to take over an account that's stopped performing, that's how we work.

Creative is the targeting

With broad audiences, the creative decides who sees the ad. Volume and variation of creative now drive results more than audience settings do.

Full-funnel, not just retargeting

Retargeting flatters the report by taking credit for demand you already created. Growth comes from the prospecting layer above it.

Tracking that survives iOS

Conversions API, server-side events and proper deduplication. Without them Meta optimises on partial data and results drift quietly.

Paid social and paid search
