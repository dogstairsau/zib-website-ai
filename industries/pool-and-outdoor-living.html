The pattern

Households research in winter, quote in spring, install in summer.

Outdoor living demand is brutally seasonal and the buying cycle runs ahead of it. Businesses that start advertising when the weather turns are already competing for the tail of a decision made months earlier.

  • You advertise in the season and buy the decision late. The high-value research happens well before the peak. Spending only in summer means paying premium rates to reach people who've already chosen.
  • Compliance is a separate, recession-proof business inside yours. Pool fencing inspection and certification is regulated, recurring and searched year-round — and it's usually buried three clicks deep under the construction pages.
  • This is a photograph-led purchase sold with stock imagery. Nobody buys a pool or a deck from a diagram. Real project photography is the single highest-impact asset in the vertical and most operators have it on a phone, unused.
Why generic marketing fails here

Generic campaigns follow demand. The money is in leading it.

Generic seasonal campaigns switch on when the weather does — exactly when clicks cost the most and decisions are already made. Here the money is in building remarketing audiences through the off-season and converting them at the peak.

Alongside it runs a separate always-on compliance line — inspections and certification — that funds the quiet months and smooths the whipsaw the construction side can't avoid.
