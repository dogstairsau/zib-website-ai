The pattern

The manufacturer wants the model sold. You need the customer through your door.

Every franchised dealership competes for attention with a national carline site that has more authority, more budget and a different objective. The manufacturer wants the model sold. You need the customer through your door rather than the dealership eleven kilometres away. Those aren't the same job and they need different search strategy.

  • You rank below your own manufacturer for your own stock. Carline sites dominate model searches. Dealer visibility comes from local intent — model plus suburb, "demo", "runout", "test drive near me" — and from a Google Business Profile most dealers treat as an afterthought.
  • Runout and plate change compress a quarter into three weeks. End-of-plate and runout periods drive enormous, brief demand spikes. Flat monthly budgets miss them entirely.
  • Service and parts are the profit centre and get no marketing at all. Fixed operations often out-earn new car sales on margin, and almost every dealer markets exclusively for new vehicles.
