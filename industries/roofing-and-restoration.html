The pattern

A hail event fills the diary for a quarter. The other eighteen months decide the business.

Roofing and restoration is the most weather-exposed vertical we work in. A hail event fills the diary for a quarter; the eighteen months either side are where the business is actually won or lost. The operators who grow are the ones with demand capture running before the storm, not scrambling after it.

  • You compete hardest exactly when everyone else does. After a storm, every roofer in the region bids the same keywords on the same day and cost per click triples. If your account isn't already warm — quality score built, negatives in place, landing pages tested — you pay the premium and lose the auction.
  • Restoration is a considered purchase sold like an emergency. A full roof restoration is a five-figure decision made over weeks by a household, often with two or three quotes. Ads that push "call now" capture the urgent tail and miss the majority who are still deciding. That audience is reachable on Meta, and almost nobody in the vertical is doing it well.
  • Your quote-to-win rate is a marketing problem. Most roofers measure leads. The number that actually moves revenue is what share of quotes convert, and that's driven by response time, how the quote is presented and what proof sits behind it.
Why generic marketing fails here

Three disciplines. Most agencies run one.

A generic agency will run a search campaign on "roof repairs" and report clicks. This vertical needs seasonal budget flexing, storm-triggered campaign activation by postcode, and a Meta remarketing layer for the long consideration window.

Those are three different disciplines, and the difference shows up in the only numbers that matter: what a lead costs during the surge, and whether the phone still rings in the quiet half of the year.

Baseline + surge structure

A standing campaign sized to your everyday capacity, and a surge layer built in advance — activated by postcode within hours of an event.

A warm account, always

Quality score, negatives and tested landing pages maintained year-round, so post-storm auctions cost you less than the roofer starting cold.

The consideration window

Meta creative — before-and-afters, finance clarity, proof — working the weeks-long restoration decision the search ads can't see.

Quote-to-win discipline

Call tracking, response-time reporting and proof assets behind the quote, because winning the job beats buying another lead.
