User Data Deletion Instructions

Zib AU Automation

Zib AU Automation is used by Zib Digital to access authorised Meta Business assets for advertising reporting, campaign management, troubleshooting and workflow automation.

How to request deletion

If you want to request deletion of data associated with this app, email data@zibdigital.com.au with:

  • Your name
  • Your Facebook profile ID or Business Manager details, if known
  • The business, Page or ad account the request relates to
  • A short description of the data deletion request

Zib Digital will review the request and delete applicable app-related data unless we are required to retain it for legal, security, billing, audit or compliance reasons.

For any questions about this app or your data, please contact:

Zib Digital Pty Ltd
Email: data@zibdigital.com.au
See also our Privacy Policy.
