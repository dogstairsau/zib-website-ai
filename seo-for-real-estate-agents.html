Real estate SEO

Stop competing with the portals. Start beating other agents.

Most agency websites are built to display listings, which is the one job the portals already do better. The commercial opportunity sits in the searches the portals rank badly for: appraisals, agent comparison, suburb market knowledge and property management.

  • Suburb authority pages. Genuine market commentary, median data and recent sales for each suburb you service. This is where vendors research and where portals are generic.
  • Appraisal and vendor intent. The high-value queries: property appraisal, what's my home worth, best agent in a suburb, commission comparison. Low volume, extraordinary value.
  • Agent profile SEO. Vendors search agents by name before they list. Your agent profiles should outrank every aggregator that has scraped them.
  • Google Business Profile and reviews. The map pack decides a large share of agency enquiries, and review volume decides the map pack.
  • Property management leads. The recurring revenue side of the business, chronically under-marketed and far less contested than sales.
  • Technical SEO for listing sites. Handling sold and withdrawn listings properly, so expired stock doesn't quietly rot your site's quality signals.
The strategic problem

Your listings are not your content.

Listings appear, sell and disappear. A site built entirely on them has no durable ranking asset, and every listing you syndicate to a portal is content that ranks for the portal instead of for you. That's the trade real estate agencies make and rarely account for.

The durable asset is suburb-level expertise published properly: what sold, what it means, what a vendor should expect. It's the one thing a portal can't produce for your patch and another agency won't bother to.

Sales vs property management

Sales SEO is brutally competitive and seasonal. Property management SEO is neither, and produces annuity revenue. Most agencies invest the wrong way round.

Individual agents

If you're an agent rather than a principal, your personal brand is the asset. Name searches, reviews and suburb content follow you between agencies.

Franchise networks

Franchise sites create structural SEO problems: duplicate content across offices and offices competing with each other. Solvable, but only deliberately.

Paid alongside organic

Appraisal keywords convert well on Google Ads and cost little, because most agents are spending their budget on portal depth instead.
