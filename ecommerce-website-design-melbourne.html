eCommerce design that converts

Pretty is table stakes. Profitable is the brief.

Most Melbourne stores we're asked to rescue don't have a traffic problem. They have a conversion problem dressed up as one. The design looks current, the checkout leaks, and nobody has read a session recording in a year.

  • Conversion-first product pages. Imagery, pricing clarity, delivery expectations, reviews and stock signals arranged in the order shoppers actually decide in.
  • Checkout and cart recovery. Fewer steps, visible costs early, express payment options, and abandonment flows that recover the sales you already paid to acquire.
  • Responsive by default. Most Melbourne eCommerce traffic is mobile and most eCommerce revenue still isn't. Mobile is where we design first and test hardest.
  • Speed as a revenue feature. Core Web Vitals, image handling and app bloat. Every second of load time is measurable in lost checkouts.
  • Search-ready architecture. Collection structure, faceted navigation, canonicals and schema built in at the design stage, not bolted on afterwards.
  • Tracking that actually reports. GA4, consent mode and server-side conversion tracking, so your ad platforms optimise against real orders.
Choosing a platform

Shopify, WooCommerce or something custom.

There's no universally right platform, only the right one for your catalogue size, margin and internal team. We build on all three and have no incentive to push you toward whichever is easiest for us.

What matters more than the platform is who owns it afterwards. You get the code, the accounts and the documentation. A store you can't leave with isn't a store you own.

Shopify

Fastest to launch, best-in-class checkout, strongest app ecosystem. The default recommendation for most Melbourne retailers under a few thousand SKUs.

WooCommerce

Best when content and commerce sit together, or when you need unusual pricing, membership or B2B logic that Shopify charges dearly to replicate.

Custom builds

For complex catalogues, ERP integration or genuinely unusual fulfilment. More expensive to build and to maintain, and worth it less often than agencies suggest.

Migrations

Replatforming is where organic revenue quietly disappears. URL mapping, redirect strategy and pre-launch crawls are non-negotiable parts of the scope, not an upsell.

A well-executed Shopify build for a Melbourne retailer typically lands somewhere in the mid five figures, with simpler catalogues below that and custom or integrated builds well above. The variables that actually move the number are catalogue complexity, how much of your product data needs cleaning, and whether you're integrating with an ERP or POS. We scope in stages so you can see the number before committing to the whole build.

Eight to sixteen weeks is typical for a considered Shopify or WooCommerce build, including discovery, design, development, content migration and testing. The single biggest cause of overrun is product data and imagery arriving late from the client side, so we front-load that request in week one.
