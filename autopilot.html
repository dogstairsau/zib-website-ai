Why this exists

The agency model was never built for a business your size.

A real agency retainer starts around three to five thousand a month, before you've spent a cent reaching anyone. If you're a founder, a start-up or a family business turning over what most Australian small businesses turn over, that's not a marketing budget — that's your marketing budget going to salaries. So you end up with one of three bad options: do it yourself badly at midnight, hand it to something cheap that quietly does nothing, or overcommit to an agency you can't really afford and hope it works before the money runs out. Autopilot is the fourth option. The same execution the big accounts get, run by an agent instead of a team, priced so the majority of your money still reaches the market.

The rules

What it does on its own. What always gets a human.

Autopilot is not an agent with your credit card and no supervision. It has an explicit list of what it may do unsupervised, and a hard stop on everything else. You'll get this list in writing before you sign anything.

Immediate · no approval needed

The agent acts, then tells you.

Anything inside the goals, budget and claims you agreed at setup happens straight away and appears in your log.

  • Pausing, resuming or restructuring campaigns
  • Moving budget between channels inside the agreed band
  • Adjusting target cost per lead toward your stated goal
  • Writing and shipping new ad creative using approved claims
  • Adding negative keywords, fixing feeds, correcting tracking
  • Publishing content and technical SEO fixes
Held · a human reviews first

The agent stops and escalates.

These never happen automatically, no matter how confidently the agent thinks it has the right answer.

  • Any new factual claim not already on your approved list
  • Superlatives, guarantees, pricing promises or comparisons
  • Spend changes beyond your agreed monthly ceiling
  • Bidding on a competitor's brand name
  • Anything touching health, finance, legal or licensing claims
  • A change of strategy rather than a change of setting
You never get handed off

When something escalates, a Zib strategist reviews it internally and the answer comes back through your agent. You are never asked to get on a call about it. No humans disappear from the business — they just stop being your problem.

Approved claims list

At setup we write down every factual claim your business is entitled to make, with the evidence behind it. The agent cannot add to that list. Nothing goes live asserting something we haven't verified.

Your money stays yours

You own your Google Ads and Meta accounts, your card stays on your own billing, and Zib holds access only. We never front, hold or invoice your ad spend — the only thing you pay us is the monthly fee.

FAQ

The questions people actually ask.

We're a small business. Is this actually for us?

It's the reason it exists. Every other option at your size is compromised: real agencies price themselves out, cheap ones take the fee and do nothing, and doing it yourself means it gets attention at eleven at night when you have the energy. Autopilot gives a $2,500-a-month business the same execution a $25,000-a-month account gets, because an agent doing the work costs roughly the same either way. What you give up is the relationship — no strategist who knows your name, no one to call. For a lot of small businesses that was never the part they were paying for anyway.

Can I pause during a quiet season?

Yes, at no charge, for as long as you need. Plenty of businesses are seasonal and it's daft to pay a management fee through the months you're not trading. Tell your agent you're going quiet, everything is paused in place, and it picks up where it left off with all your history intact. No restart fee, no renegotiation.

Is there really no human involved?

There are humans — you just never have to deal with them. Zib strategists review anything the agent escalates, verify new claims, and keep an eye on the accounts. The promise isn't that nobody is there. The promise is that you are never asked to attend a meeting, take a call, or manage a relationship to get your marketing done.

Why is setup still a real conversation?

Because it's the one part that can't be automated well, and everything downstream depends on it. In ninety minutes we check your conversion tracking actually works, agree what a customer is worth to you, write down which claims your business can legitimately make, and set the ceiling and boundaries the agent operates inside. Skip it and you get an agent confidently optimising toward the wrong number — which is exactly how small accounts get quietly wasted. It's the highest-leverage ninety minutes in the product, and it's why there's a one-off setup fee.

How do I ask for something?

Talk to your agent directly in the browser, or just reply to any email it sends you — both land in the same place, with the full history of your account behind them. It remembers what you've tried, what failed, what you liked and what you told it never to do again. Ask it to pause something, change direction, explain a decision, or justify last month's spend, and you get an answer straight away rather than a diary invitation.

What stops the agent doing something stupid with my budget?

A hard ceiling you set at setup, which the agent cannot exceed without a human approving it. Within that ceiling it can move money between channels and campaigns; beyond it, it stops and escalates. Spend pacing is monitored continuously, and anomalies break through to a human within the hour rather than waiting for a batch review.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, directly. Your Google Ads and Meta accounts stay in your name with your card on the billing, and Zib holds access only. We never front your spend, never hold it, and never invoice it. The only money that moves between us is the monthly fee. It keeps your costs transparent and it keeps you in control of the tap.

Will an AI write something embarrassing or untrue about my business?

It's the risk we've designed hardest against. Every piece of copy is checked against your approved claims list before it can go live, and the agent is not permitted to add anything to that list. If copy asserts a fact we haven't verified — an award, a response time, a "number one" — it's blocked and sent to a human. Superlatives, guarantees and comparative claims are blocked by default regardless.

What if it doesn't work?

Cancel any month, no exit fee, and you keep the accounts, creative, content and pages. We won't guarantee a specific number of leads because nobody honestly can. We do guarantee the work ships, the log is complete and the response times hold — and if we miss on those, that month is refunded.

Why is there a minimum ad spend at all?

Because below about a thousand dollars a month there isn't enough data for anyone — human or agent — to optimise against, and the fee ends up being most of what you spend. That's a bad deal for you and it produces a bad result we'd get blamed for. The minimum protects you more than it protects us. If you're under it, put the money into ads directly for a few months and come back when there's a budget worth managing.

Can I move up to Managed AI later?

Yes, and a good number of clients do once their account is big enough to warrant a strategist. Everything transfers, because it's the same accounts and the same team behind it. Autopilot isn't a walled-off product, it's the first rung.

What if I need more work than my plan includes?

Extra work is either queued to the following month or quoted, your choice — and the agent will tell you which way it's leaning before anything is charged. Conversation is never metered, only shipped output. If you're consistently pushing past your plan, that's usually a sign you'd be better off a tier up, and the agent will say so.
