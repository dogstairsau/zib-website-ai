Local vs National SEO

Local SEO targets local clients with geographically focused keywords, aiming to increase brand visibility in a specific area. It's cost-effective and has less competition.

National reach

National SEO targets customers nationwide, ideal for online businesses or those with no location constraints. It requires broader keywords, faces intense competition and is more challenging to rank.

The main difference between push and pull marketing lies in how consumers are approached. The idea behind push marketing is to promote products by pushing them onto people. Examples include radio, television and social media advertising. Push marketing is great for developing brand awareness. While it does generate leads, it's not as effective as pull marketing for creating conversions. Pull marketing draws consumers to the products, and as research shows, has much higher conversion rates. Google Ads and SEO are great examples of pull marketing.

These two digital marketing strategies differ in terms of objectives. SEO is a process of improving organic positions on various search engines. On the other hand, PPC (pay-per-click) focuses on investing a budget for advertising and promoting your website or products. PPC gives quick results, whereas SEO takes time. Both strategies are very effective, especially when used together.
