        Common questions

        Things people ask before they upload.

        Are these actually finished ads?

        Yes. Each creative is composited at Meta's native sizes (1080×1080, 1080×1350, 1080×1920) with your photos, colours, logo and fonts, then downloads as a PNG you can load straight into Ads Manager or post organically.

        Why do I upload my own fonts?

        Because your brand's typeface is licensed to you, not to us. You upload the files you already own, the creatives render with the real thing, and no font ever gets re-hosted or substituted.

        What happens to my photos?

        The creatives are rendered in your browser. A copy of your kit is stored securely so the strategist who calls you has the same assets in front of them — it's never shared or reused elsewhere.

        Who owns the ads?

        You do. They're built from your own assets. Run them, hand them to another agency, post them organically — no strings.

        What if my photos aren't professional?

        Phone photos work if they're sharp and well-lit. The layouts do the design heavy lifting — a clean product shot on a bench beats a blurry studio attempt every time.

        Will you try to sell me something?

        A senior strategist will walk you through the pack and show you what running it properly would cost. If you want help, great. If not, you've still got 12 ads.
