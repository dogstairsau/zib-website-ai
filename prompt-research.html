The playbook

What you do with the "maybe" or "no" results.

Every gap is a concrete fix. For each prompt where your brand isn't likely to show up in the AI's answer, here's how you close it.

01 · Content

Authoritative content

Write the deep guide on that specific question. AI training data picks up genuine subject-matter authority — surface-level pages don't make the cut.

02 · Citations

Citations from sites AI trusts

Industry publications, Wikipedia, government sites, .edu, news. AI weights these heavily when forming its "who is reputable in this category" map.

03 · Community

Reddit / Quora / forums

Heavy training data source. If competitors are recommended in r/AusFinance threads about your category and your client isn't, that's a fix.

04 · Schema

Schema markup

Organization, Service, FAQ and Product schemas help AI correctly attribute facts to your brand and reduce ambiguity.

05 · Local

GMB + local citations

Critical for anything geo-bound — "plumber Brighton", "lawyer Sandringham". AI defaults to the strongest local signals it can verify.

06 · Tracking

Re-run monthly

AI training data updates on a multi-month cycle. Re-running this tool tracks whether the work is actually moving the visibility needle.

FAQ

Common questions.

What does this tool actually do?+

It generates the prompts real users would type into ChatGPT or Claude about your category, classifies the intent behind each (Informational, Commercial, Navigational, Transactional), and — if you add a target domain — predicts whether that brand would show up in the AI's answer.

It mines Google autosuggest under the hood, so the prompts are grounded in things people actually search for, not hypothetical questions.

What's the difference between SEO and AEO?+

SEO optimises for blue links on Google. You win when your page ranks #1 and the user clicks through.

AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) optimises for direct AI answers — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Google's AI Overviews. You win when the AI names your brand in its response, often before the user ever sees a list of links.

SEO is now table stakes. AEO is the new battleground, and most agencies haven't moved into it yet.

Why does it matter if my brand isn't named in AI answers?+

A growing share of searches end at the AI's answer — the user never scrolls down to the blue links. If you're not named in the answer, you're effectively invisible, even if you rank #1 on Google for the same query.

This shift is happening fastest on commercial and informational queries — exactly where buying decisions are formed.

How accurate is the visibility prediction?+

The yes / maybe / no rating is each model's own assessment of whether a leading AI would surface your domain. It's a strong directional signal based on what the model knows about your brand, industry, geography and content authority.

Treat it as a starting hypothesis. Validate by typing the prompt directly into ChatGPT or Claude and watching what actually shows up. Where the prediction and reality agree, you have a confirmed gap to close.

What do I do about a "no" result?+

Work through the six-step playbook above. The fastest wins are usually deep, authoritative content on the specific question, plus citations from sites the AI already trusts (industry publications, .gov, .edu, Wikipedia, major news).

For local-intent prompts ("plumber Brighton"), the biggest lever is usually Google Business Profile + local citations.

How often should I re-run this?+

Monthly during an active AEO push, quarterly once you're holding ground. AI training data updates on a multi-month cadence, so visibility shifts on roughly the same timeline as your content and citation work compounds.

Can I track competitors?+

Yes — enter a competitor's domain instead of (or in addition to) your own. Same seed term, different domain, and you'll see which intent buckets they own versus where they have gaps too.

Does this only work for Australian businesses?+

The Google autosuggest layer is locale-bound to Australia, so the long-tail questions reflect what Australians ask. The AI visibility assessment works globally — the model evaluates the domain regardless of geography.
