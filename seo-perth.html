Search engine optimisation in Perth

The whole state searches from here.

Perth is the most isolated capital in the country, and search reflects it. Western Australia's population is concentrated here, and a Perth business is very often the supplier for the Pilbara, the Goldfields, the South West and the Wheatbelt as well. "Perth" in a search is frequently a stand-in for "Western Australia", and a keyword plan that stops at the metro boundary leaves that demand to somebody else.

The competition is also unusual. The local agency field is smaller than Melbourne's or Sydney's, but national brands and east-coast agencies target Perth terms from a distance, on templated pages with a Perth heading and nothing else local about them. That is the gap a Perth business wins in: WA-specific content, reviews from people who live here, and a Google Business Profile that is actually maintained.

Then there is the shape of the city itself. Perth runs a long way north to south, and buyers north of the river and south of it behave like separate markets. We build from the suburb and service searches you can win, then extend to the regional terms your business can genuinely supply.

SEO for a new website

A new Perth domain faces fewer local incumbents than the east-coast capitals, but national brands hold many of the head terms. The first six months are foundations, suburb and service coverage, and the WA-specific content the nationals never write.

Taking over an existing site

Many Perth sites we see were built by an east-coast agency from a template with a Perth H1. We audit what's there, keep what ranks, and replace the pages that read as if nobody involved had been west of Adelaide.

Local vs Perth-wide

A plumber in Joondalup gains nothing from ranking in Rockingham. Suburb pages and the map pack bring the customers who can use you. If you supply the Pilbara or the South West, the plan covers the state from the start.

Paid alongside organic

Google Ads buys position across WA while SEO earns it, and shows within weeks which regions and services produce enquiries. Our Perth automotive client is the proof: over a thousand leads at under ten dollars each.
