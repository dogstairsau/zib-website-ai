Kicking Off a Winning Partnership: Zib Digital Joins Forces with Melbourne Victory

At Zib Digital, we're thrilled to announce our partnership with Melbourne Victory as their Premier Digital Marketing Partner for the 2024/25 season. This exciting collaboration brings together two organisations sharing a passion for innovation, community engagement, branding and digital marketing.

As a leading digital marketing agency based in Australia and passionate football supporters, we're proud to support Melbourne Victory's vision to Lead, Unite, Connect and Inspire.

As an agency, we have a large and devoted team of diverse experts with decades of refined experience within the digital marketing industry. Our expertise in connecting brands with their audiences through data-driven strategies and creative solutions will help amplify the Club's online presence and deepen connections with fans.

"We're honoured to partner with Melbourne Victory, one of Australia's most iconic football clubs," said Nicole Wagenecht, our Chief Growth Officer at Zib Digital. "Our team is committed to delivering innovative digital marketing solutions that bring fans closer to the action and create more impactful experiences."

Through this partnership, we'll work closely with Melbourne Victory to enhance fan engagement, develop meaningful digital interactions and amplify the Club's online presence. Our goal is to drive business growth through strategic marketing initiatives that resonate with Melbourne Victory's dedicated fan base.

Melbourne Victory's Managing Director, Caroline Carnegie, echoed our excitement: "We're excited to welcome Zib Digital as our Premier Digital Marketing Partner. Their commitment to digital innovation aligns perfectly with our vision."

At Zib Digital, we're passionate about helping organisations like Melbourne Victory achieve digital success. Our team of experts is dedicated to delivering results-driven marketing solutions that drive real and lasting growth.

Stay tuned for updates on our partnership and the exciting initiatives we'll be launching with Melbourne Victory.
