Full-service email marketing

Not a newsletter. A revenue channel.

Most email programmes we inherit are one monthly send to the whole list. The money is in the automated flows nobody has built: the sequences that fire off behaviour and keep producing revenue while you're asleep.

  • Lifecycle flows. Welcome sequences, abandoned cart and browse, post-purchase, replenishment and win-back. Built once, earning continuously.
  • Segmentation that means something. Behaviour, purchase history and engagement recency, rather than one list treated as one person.
  • Campaign management. Planned, written, designed, tested and sent, on a calendar that ties to your commercial year rather than to whatever occurred to someone on Monday.
  • Deliverability engineering. SPF, DKIM and DMARC, list hygiene and sender reputation. An email that lands in Promotions was never going to convert.
  • Design and copy. Templates that render properly in Outlook and on mobile, with copy written to be scanned rather than read.
  • Reporting on revenue. Revenue per email, per flow and per subscriber. Open rates stopped being trustworthy the day Apple Mail Privacy Protection shipped.
Where email fits

The channel that makes the others profitable.

Email rarely acquires the customer. It decides whether that customer was worth acquiring. When your paid media has to fund a business on first purchase alone, the numbers are brutal. When email reliably produces the second and third purchase, the whole acquisition model changes.

That's why we don't run email as a standalone retainer in isolation. It sits alongside the paid and organic work, sharing the same audience data and the same commercial targets.

Platform agnostic

Klaviyo, Mailchimp, HubSpot, Campaign Monitor, ActiveCampaign. We work in what you already pay for unless there's a real reason to move.

eCommerce vs B2B

Retail email lives on flows and frequency. B2B email lives on nurture and sales alignment. Same channel, genuinely different playbooks.

Spam Act compliance

Australian rules on consent, sender identification and unsubscribe are stricter than a lot of imported templates assume. We build to them.

List growth

An email programme is capped by the size and quality of the list. Capture strategy on-site and through paid social is part of the work, not an afterthought.
