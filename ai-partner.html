FAQ

Common questions, straight answers.

Does this replace our marketing team?

No, it multiplies them. Your squad takes the legwork, your people make the calls, and a senior Zib specialist coaches them along the way.

Can it work alongside our agency?

Yes. We map who does what during discovery, so the squad supports your team and your agency rather than duplicating work.

Which platforms does it work with?

Commonly Google Ads, Meta, Google Analytics 4, Search Console, your CRM (HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce, Zoho) and your website platform (WordPress, Webflow, Shopify and others). The exact connections are confirmed during discovery. If your stack isn't listed, ask.

How is our brand voice maintained?

Your brand rules, tone of voice and approved examples are built into the squad's knowledge, and every draft is reviewed before anything is published.

Who approves campaigns and content?

You do: your team, or a senior Zib specialist where agreed. Nothing goes out without your OK.

How long before the first specialists are live?

Timing is agreed during discovery and depends on the scope and the systems involved. Starting with the core five (Strategy, Content, Google Ads, SEO and Reporting) is the quickest path.

How much of my team's time does it take?

Regular reviews, plus signing off what your squad drafts. Your team learns the system while it's being set up, so it knows how it works from day one.

Who are the senior specialists?

Senior paid media, SEO and content people from Zib's own team, the same people running campaigns for Zib clients every day. Meet the team →

Who owns our data, workflows and outputs?

Your data stays yours. Ownership of workflows and outputs is set out clearly in your agreement before anything is built.

Is our data safe?

Your squad works inside your tools, with access scoped to what each specialist needs. We sign NDAs as standard and use enterprise-tier model providers with no-training data agreements.

What does it cost?

A one-off setup, then a monthly fee, both scoped to the specialists you start with and the tools involved. We map it on your scoping call, and most teams find the comparison with a new hire is the persuasive part.

What's the difference between this and Managed AI?

Managed AI = we run it for you, and you focus on the business. AI Partner = your squad works alongside your team, your people sign off, and we coach them every month. More on Managed AI →
