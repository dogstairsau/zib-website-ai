FAQ

Common questions, straight answers.

Who actually owns the agent and the data?

You do. The agent is built for your business on Zib's system and flow architecture, and runs on your own private server, connected to your CMS, CRM and data. You'll need your own ChatGPT account for the agent to run against, so model usage stays yours. Your data and logs live in your environment, and if we part ways the agent keeps running.

How long does it take to build the first agent?

Four weeks from kickoff to live. Our agents are built on Zib's own framework, we're not architecting from scratch each time, we're configuring proven infrastructure to your stack and your data.

What tools and stacks do you build into?

We're tech-agnostic. CMS: WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Sanity, Contentful, Ghost. CRM: HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce, Zoho. Models: Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini. Orchestration: n8n, Zapier, Make, custom Node. If your stack isn't here, ask, we've probably built into it.

What if I outgrow the partnership?

That's the goal. By month nine to twelve, most clients are running their agents day-to-day with us as a sounding board. We're happy to step back to a quarterly check-in or hand over fully, the agent is yours either way.

Is my data safe?

Agents live in your environment, behind your auth. We sign NDAs as standard, scope data access tightly, and use enterprise-tier model providers with no-training data agreements. We can also build into self-hosted models if you're regulated.

Can I add or remove agents over time?

Yes. Most clients start with one agent, add a second within four months, and a third by month nine. Operator and Scale tiers include agents at a flat per-agent rate. Decommissioning an agent is simple, we hand you a wind-down doc and remove the integration.

What's the difference between this and Managed AI?

Managed AI = we run agents in our environment, you focus on the business. AI Partner = we build agents in your environment, you own them. Managed is faster to launch and lower-touch. Partner is slower upfront but builds an asset on your balance sheet. More on Managed AI →
