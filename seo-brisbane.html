Search engine optimisation in Brisbane

Brisbane searches like a region, not a city.

Brisbane's search market behaves differently from Sydney's or Melbourne's. The customers for a Brisbane business are spread across South East Queensland, from Ipswich and Logan to Moreton Bay and the Redlands, and often down to the Gold Coast and up to the Sunshine Coast. Most of the businesses we work with here are service-area businesses, not single-suburb ones, and the keyword plan has to reflect that.

The second difference is the state of the competition. Brisbane is growing quickly, and demand is arriving faster than many of the local sites serving it have improved. A good share of the businesses holding the top positions are there on thin, dated pages. Well-executed fundamentals move faster here than in the southern capitals.

In practice that means we map your real service area across South East Queensland before writing a page, build the suburb and service coverage the map pack rewards, and then compete for the Brisbane head terms from a position of authority rather than hope.

SEO for a new website

A new Brisbane domain has no history, but the field is thinner than the southern capitals. Six months of foundations and service-area coverage can put a new site in front of incumbents that stopped investing years ago.

Taking over an existing site

Most Brisbane accounts we inherit have location pages for suburbs the business never visits and links from directories nobody uses. We audit and prune before adding anything.

Local vs Brisbane-wide

A roofer in Moreton Bay and a wholesaler shipping across Queensland need different keyword plans. We map targets to where you can actually service, which for most Brisbane businesses is a region, not a suburb.

Paid alongside organic

Google Ads buys position across South East Queensland while SEO earns it, and tells you within weeks which suburbs and services produce revenue. That data decides where the SEO effort goes next.
