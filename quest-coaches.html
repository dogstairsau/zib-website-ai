What's wrong

Your website isn't broken. It was never built to win work.

Every page loads, nothing is missing, and Google hasn't punished you. What you have is a brochure from about ten years ago that says you own buses. It doesn't answer what a school, a business or a club asks before booking, so you've simply been out-built.

Someone on their phone can't call you

Normally a phone turns a number on a website into a button you tap to call. Your site specifically tells it not to, and there's no tap-to-call link to make up for it. So the 1300 number sits there as plain text — to ring you, someone has to memorise it, leave your site and dial manually. Your site was also never updated for phones, so it loads shrunken and that number is half the size it should be.

Crown and Ventura both have two tappable numbers on every page. This is the cheapest fix on the list and the one costing you the most.

And you can't see where your work comes from

Your visitor tracking stopped collecting anything in 2023, and quotes happen over on your separate booking system. So nobody can tell you which suburb, service or search brought last month's bookings in. That gets fixed in the first fortnight, because everything after it is measured against it.

Two smaller things worth knowing: one of your pages is titled “Crown Bus Charter”, a competitor's name, which is why you turn up when people search for Crown. And there's still a COVID notice on every page with a footer that says 2021.

And this is costing you school work

Victorian education rules mean a school booking a private coach has to satisfy itself the operator is properly accredited. Your site gives them nothing to check — your about page says “fully accredited” and stops there. A business manager comparing three quotes finds all of this on Crown's site and none of it on yours.

How 36 new pages get written without cutting corners

We design three layouts once — trip pages, area pages, bus pages — then fill each one with its own words, photos, questions and reviews. The layout repeats. The words never do. Google is very good now at spotting pages that are the same thing with the names swapped, and it buries them, which is why this runs over twelve months instead of one week.

Same reason we're not building a page per suburb. Nine area pages go in, each with real detail, and we add more only where we can see people searching.

Nothing gets deleted. Old addresses forward to the new ones so you keep what you've already earned.

Months 7–12

Build reputation, chase the big searches

  • Six guides, one a month, written to get you linked to and quoted
  • Steady work getting other websites to link to yours, from three upwards
  • The big competitive searches, now there's something behind them
  • Report every month, a proper sit-down every three, next year's plan in month eleven
What we won't do is promise you a traffic number today. You've had no working data since 2023, and four visitors a month is too small a base to forecast from honestly. We count four things from day one — phone taps, quote forms, portal quotes and emails, each traced back to the page and search that produced it — then set targets at the end of month one against real numbers. Anyone quoting you visitor figures for this site right now is guessing.
