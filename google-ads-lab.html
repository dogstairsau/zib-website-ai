          Common questions

          Things people ask before they drop the URL.

          Is the pack free?

          Yes. The audit, the search ad, the keyword themes and the Performance Max assets are free. We make money when brands ask a Premier Partner to run their Google Ads, not before.

          How do you see my current ads?

          We check the Google Ads Transparency Center for your domain, which shows the ads an advertiser is running. We give you the direct link so you can see them too.

          Can I load these straight into Google Ads?

          Yes. Every asset is written inside Google's character limits, so the search ad and Performance Max group drop in cleanly. A strategist will pressure-test the structure first.

          Who owns the assets?

          You do. Take them, load them, hand them to another agency. The work is yours.

          What if my site is thin or new?

          We lean harder on category convention and keyword intent. The structure still holds; the strategist sharpens it on the call.

          Will you try to sell me something?

          We'll show you what running this properly would cost. If you want help, great. If not, you've still got the pack.
