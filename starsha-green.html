Common questions

Questions we actually get asked.

01

What does "Zib partner" actually mean?

Starsha operates as a Zib partner. She's not a Zib employee. Her P&L is tied to her territory and her clients. The incentive model is structurally different from any other agency in the Zib network: when your campaigns work, her business grows. When they don't, hers suffers directly. That's why accountability isn't a slogan on this page.

02

Will I be working with Starsha directly, or a junior?

Starsha owns the relationship, the strategic direction and the accountability. A dedicated senior digital manager handles the day-to-day execution across channels, briefed and reviewed by Starsha. So you get two senior brains on your account: partner-level commercial thinking plus senior specialist craft. No juniors running things behind the scenes, no handoff to a service rep.

03

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Trades, hospitality, manufacturers, professional services, commercial B2B, local retail, ecommerce operators. If the business has a commercial outcome tied to being found on Google (phone calls, form fills, quote requests, online sales), the model applies. Starsha has owned and operated in hospitality herself, so she knows what a marketing dollar has to bring back.

04

How long does SEO take to show results?

Technical fixes move the needle in weeks. Content and local rankings compound over three to six months. Anyone promising "page one in 30 days" for commercial keywords is either lying or bidding on your brand name. Starsha reports weekly so you see the compounding, not just the finish line.

05

Where does Starsha work?

Starsha works with clients across Australia from her Mornington Peninsula base. If you're based here but sell nationally or interstate, her team builds the local and national architecture together. If you're outside the region, the Zib partner for your area handles you directly. Same model, different face.
