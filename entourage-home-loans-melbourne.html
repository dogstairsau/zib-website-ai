Before you ask

The questions we get every week.

What does a broker cost me?

Nothing, in almost every case. Brokers are paid a commission by the lender whose loan you choose, and it doesn't change the rate you're offered. If a scenario ever needs a fee — some complex commercial or specialist lending does — we tell you the number before any work starts, never after.

Will this affect my credit score?

No. The call and the comparison work happen without touching your credit file. A credit enquiry is only made once you've picked a lender and told us to lodge the application.

Do I have to leave my current bank?

No. Sometimes the best result is a repriced loan with the bank you're already with, and if that's what the comparison shows, that's what we'll tell you. We'd rather be right than move you.

I'm not in Melbourne. Can you still help?

Yes. Entourage is Melbourne-based but licensed to arrange finance Australia-wide, with offices in Cremorne, Mornington, Geelong, Brisbane and Byron Bay, and clients overseas. Our buyers advocacy service is the one that's Victoria-only.

How long does it take?

A first call within one business day, options back to you in a few days, and a formal approval typically inside two to four weeks depending on the lender and how quickly documents come through. If you're under a contract deadline, say so on the call and we'll work the timeline backwards from it.

What if I'm just looking at the moment?

Then say so in the form and we'll skip the broker call. You'll get the calculators and the guides instead, which are more use at that stage anyway — and you're welcome to come back to us whenever there's a real deadline attached.
