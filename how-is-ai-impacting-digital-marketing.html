How is AI Impacting Digital Marketing?

In the ever evolving realm of digital marketing, the relationship between artificial intelligence (AI) and human emotion and intellect is reshaping how businesses connect with their audiences. As a leading digital marketing agency in Australia, we’re proud to be at the forefront of this digital revolution. In this blog, we're breaking down how AI is impacting search engine optimisation (SEO), pay-per-click advertising (PPC) and social media, exploring both the transformative potential and the evolving challenges that arise as AI continues to shape the landscape of digital marketing.

Google values content that serves the audience, whether it's crafted by human hands or fine-tuned by AI algorithms. In fact, in a recent iteration of Google's Helpful Content Update, the phrase ‘written by people’ has been replaced by a statement that the search engine is constantly monitoring ‘content created for people’ to rank sites. The new language shows that Google recognises AI as a tool used in content creation. But instead of simply focusing on distinguishing AI from human content, the search engine wants to highlight valuable content that benefits users, regardless of whether humans or machines produced it. Google has further clarified that they are not against AI content, meaning there is no penalty for utilising AI content.

Regardless of the creator, meeting certain standards is crucial for content to rank well in search engines like Google. The E-E-A-T guidelines (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness and Trustworthiness) serve as benchmarks, which Google's algorithms use to assess content based, irrespective of whether it was initially drafted by AI or a human writer. Adhering to these guidelines ensures that content demonstrates expertise, authoritativeness and trustworthiness on its subject matter, creator credentials and overall reliability. By following these guidelines, content is more likely to secure a favourable position in search engine results pages (SERPs), regardless of who (or what) wrote it.

So, does this mean AI can be tasked with handling all the copywriting and on-page optimisation tasks now? Definitely not and it’s crucial to understand the importance of balance, with AI’s role in SEO being just one element of a sophisticated strategy. With readily available AI-powered software, marketers can certainly improve their content production efforts without compromising quality. However, it should only form one part of a comprehensive approach - meticulous research into client industries, competitor landscapes and user intent form the backbone of a holistic strategy.

There is no doubt that AI is revolutionising the landscape of paid advertising and it has been doing so for a number of years now. As the field of pay-per-click (PPC) advertising is continuously evolving, AI is playing a pivotal role in shaping its future. Here’s just some of the ways AI is impacting PPC:

With enhanced targeting capabilities, AI algorithms can analyse vast amounts of data to discern patterns and insights that may elude conventional methods. This results in more precise audience targeting, ensuring ads are strategically displayed to users who are most likely to express interest in the promoted product or service.

The automation of bid optimisation in real time, guided by AI driven tools, further refines ad spend allocation, considering factors such as user behaviour, time of day and device used. This dynamic approach maximises return on investment (ROI) by channelling resources to the most effective avenues.

AI's impact extends beyond efficient resource allocation - it influences the creation of more relevant and compelling ads by leveraging insights into user preferences and behaviours, leading to higher click-through rates (CTRs) and conversion rates.

The integration of predictive analytics, fuelled by machine learning algorithms, can empower marketers to anticipate future trends and proactively refine their PPC strategies, enhancing overall campaign performance.

You may be asking if this means we can just sit back and revel in the glory of machine learning? Not quite! While AI brings numerous advantages, it also presents certain challenges and considerations and should be viewed as a tool that complements human expertise, not one that replaces it. AI can take the reins in various aspects but it's crucial for marketers to maintain a guiding hand, especially in tasks like content creation and strategic decision making, ensuring the right balance of AI efficiency and human creativity and insight.

Social Media: Where AI Enhances, Humans Engage

Using AI in social media offers several advantages which can be leveraged to achieve better results. Firstly, AI powered tools are proving instrumental in automating various tasks, from generating creative ideas to optimising content creation and analysing performance metrics. This not only saves valuable time for marketing teams but also eliminates the barriers posed by creative blocks, fostering a more dynamic and responsive content creation process.

Beyond streamlining processes, AI in social media provides a notable advantage in audience targeting. AI powered tools delve into user behaviour, demographics and interests to pinpoint the most relevant audience for a specific campaign. This granular analysis empowers businesses to deliver content tailored to resonate with their target audience, ultimately driving increased engagement and conversion rates.

As AI technology continues to advance, we anticipate the emergence of even more innovative applications and use cases, driving social media marketing into new possibilities. But while AI plays a role in social media, it cannot replace the irreplaceable - the human touch. In the hands of social media experts, AI becomes a tool for enhancement rather than a replacement. The human touch, driven by intuition and creativity, ensures authentic engagement with audiences. A successful approach goes beyond algorithms - it's about resonating authentically with followers. This combination of AI capabilities and human insight enables digital marketers to curate content that not only captures attention but also builds lasting connections.
