AdWords management services

Not clicks. Cost per acquisition.

Most AdWords accounts we inherit are optimised for the wrong number. Impression share looks healthy, click-through rate looks fine, and the business still can't say what a customer costs. We start at the bottom of the funnel and work back.

  • Account restructure. Campaign and ad group architecture rebuilt around margin and intent, not around how the account happened to grow.
  • Search term hygiene. Weekly negative keyword work. Most inherited accounts are paying for a third of their traffic twice, or paying for traffic that was never going to buy.

  • Ad copy and asset testing. Responsive search assets tested continuously, with the losing variants actually removed.
  • Landing page conversion work. The cheapest performance gain in most accounts isn't in the auction, it's on the page the click lands on.

The fundamentals

What AdWords management actually involves.

Google Ads is an auction, and the auction never stops moving. Competitors change bids, Google ships new formats, and match types quietly broaden. An account left alone for a quarter is an account losing money slowly.

Managed AdWords means someone senior is in the account every week: reading search terms, moving budget between campaigns, killing the ad groups that flatter the report but never produce a sale. That's the difference between a managed account and a monitored one.

AdWords or Google Ads?

The same platform. Google retired the AdWords name in 2018, but the search demand for "AdWords management" never went away, and neither did the discipline behind it.
