Quick detour · What is schema, actually?

Schema is name
tags on your burrito.

Stay with us. We’re using the word “schema” a lot in this proposal. Here’s the whole thing in plain English so the rest reads cleanly.

Mad Mex Bondi Junction
TypeRestaurant
CuisineMexican
HalalYes ✓
Open11am – 9pm
Burrito$13.90 · 620 cal
DeliveryYes ✓
AddressBondi Junction NSW

Imagine you made a burrito for Google.

You know it has rice, chicken, salsa verde, sour cream. But to Google’s robots, it’s just a tinfoil cylinder. They can guess what’s inside — but they might guess wrong, or guess slowly, or hand the answer to someone else.

Schema is the sticker on the foil. “Burrito. Chicken. Halal. 620 calories. $13.90. Bondi Junction. Open until 9pm. Delivery yes.” Now Google knows exactly what it’s looking at — and so does ChatGPT, and so does Google AI Overviews.

On a website, schema is invisible code that does the same job. It tells Google “this is a restaurant, this is the address, this is the menu, this is halal, this location does delivery, the burrito costs $13.90, here’s how to order”.

Without it, Google guesses from the page text and AI engines often skip Mad Mex entirely. With it, Mad Mex Bondi shows up in the Local Pack, your menu prices appear in search results, AI Overviews quote you directly when someone asks “is Mad Mex halal?”, and your delivery hours pop up automatically.

Right now Mad Mex has FAQ schema on two pages (good) and Restaurant / LocalBusiness / Menu schema on zero pages (bad). Fixing that is fix number one in the build-out below.

The build-out

Eight fixes that turn
the site into a ranking asset.

Quick wins first — the technical foundations the audit flagged as Priority 1. Then the strategic content layer. All inside the engagement.

01

LocalBusiness schema · all 60+ stores

Restaurant / LocalBusiness JSON-LD on every store page — address, hours, geo, cuisine, halal flag, phone. Single biggest local-pack lever on the site.

02

Fix H1s & meta descriptions site-wide

Homepage H1 currently points to a campaign banner. Location pages have no H1. FAQ and Blog have no meta description. All fixed in pass one.

03

Menu + Restaurant schema

Schema.org Menu & MenuItem markup on /our-menu — unlocks rich results and AI Overview citations for food/nutrition queries.

04

Image alt text pass

27 of 36 homepage images are missing alt text. Site-wide sweep with descriptive, keyword-aware alt for every food, hero and store image.

05

City landing pages

/sydney, /melbourne, /brisbane, /perth — aggregate-LocalBusiness schema, city-specific copy, internal linking to every store in the city.

06

Dietary & halal hub

Standalone pages for halal, vegan, gluten-free and kids/family. FAQ schema on each. Captures dietary search + answers AI engines directly.

07

FAQ rebuild for AEO

Replace app/loyalty FAQs with the 30 questions Australians actually ask AI engines about Mexican food. Schema-marked, structured for citation.

08

Active digital PR + links

Replace the 62 referring domains the site lost last period. Food media outreach, halal directories, franchise/business press, community sponsorships.
