Signs your roof needs attention

Don't wait for it to come through the ceiling

Brown or yellow water stains

Stains on ceilings or cornices mean water is already getting past the roof.

Paint bubbling or peeling

Moisture trapped behind paint is often a slow leak or failed flashing.

Sagging ceiling

Water sitting on plasterboard is serious. Call before it gives way.

Mould or moss

Damp roof spaces and blocked gutters under trees breed mould and moss.

Gutters overflowing in rain

Undersized or blocked box gutters overflow straight into the house.

Rust, lifting sheets or loose tiles

An ageing roof gets worse with every storm. Replacing it early costs less.

Common questions

Before you call

How much does a re-roof cost?

It depends on the roof's size and pitch, access, the material you choose and what needs replacing underneath. We inspect first and give you a detailed written quote with a fixed price before any work starts.

Can you find a leak other roofers couldn't?

Often, yes. Leaks rarely start where the stain is. We trace the water back through the roof, flashings and box gutters to the real cause, rather than patching the symptom.

Repair or replace: how do I know?

If the sheets are sound and the problem is local, a repair makes sense. If there's widespread rust, repeated leaks or failing box gutters, replacing is usually cheaper over time. We'll tell you honestly which it is.

Do you handle box gutters on old Victorian homes?

Yes. Box gutter replacement on period homes, often with skylights and earlier patch jobs, is a large part of our work.

Is the work certified?

All roofing is completed by licensed roof plumbers (VBA licence 116917) to Victorian Building Authority requirements, with compliance certificates issued where required.
