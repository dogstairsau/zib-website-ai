04 The plan

Go narrow before going wide. Two ways in. Same $2,500 budget.

Of that $2,500, only $2,000 is going to Google Ads (the other $500 runs Meta retargeting). Spread $2,000 across two cities and two services and your ad will barely show up in any of them. We'd rather pick one fight on Google, win it, then expand from data. Two ways to do that — either works.
