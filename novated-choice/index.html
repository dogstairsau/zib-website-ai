Is this actually for you?

A novated lease works best if you're…

It isn't right for everyone, and we'll say so. But if most of these sound like you, the savings are usually substantial.

A permanent employee

Working full-time or part-time on a regular PAYG income. Your employer doesn't need an existing scheme — we'll set it up for them.

After a genuine tax saving

Paying for the car and its running costs from pre-tax salary lowers your taxable income — and takes the GST off the purchase price.

Someone who values one bill

Finance, insurance, rego, servicing, tyres and fuel or charging — one payment, one date, no admin.

Considering an EV

Eligible electric vehicles are exempt from Fringe Benefits Tax, which makes them the single biggest saving available under a novated lease.

Wanting an exit that suits you

At the end of the term you keep it, sell it, extend, or upgrade to something new. The choice is yours, not the financier's.

Short on time

You choose the car. We handle the lender panel, the employer paperwork, the insurance and the delivery.

The lease belongs to you, not your employer, so the car goes with you. You can transfer the lease to your new employer (subject to their approval), pay out the remaining balance to own it, or keep making payments personally until you start somewhere new. We handle the transfer paperwork.

Novated lease agreements typically run from one to five years (12 to 60 months). Most people choose three to five years, which balances flexibility against lower repayments. Longer terms mean smaller payments but a larger residual at the end.

You choose. Pay the residual and own the car outright, sell it and keep anything above the residual, extend the lease if you're eligible, or trade up into something new. We'll walk you through the options a few months before the term ends so there are no surprises.

Eligible electric vehicles under the luxury car tax threshold for fuel-efficient vehicles are exempt from Fringe Benefits Tax. In practice that means the entire lease payment can come out of your pre-tax salary, rather than part of it being post-tax. On a mid-priced EV that difference is usually worth many thousands of dollars a year, on top of the GST saving.
