The big idea · in plain English

The lemonade stand test.

Imagine two kids selling the exact same cup of lemonade for $2.

One kid is friendly, remembers your name, and her stand always looks great. You'd happily pay $5 at her stand. She still only charges $2. That extra $3 of "happy" is the value her brand creates.

All summer, people walk past the other stand to line up at hers. Same lemonade. Stronger brand.

That is exactly what a brand does for a business: it makes people want you more, pay you more, and come back. This tool measures how strong that effect is for you, and turns it into a score.

Why it matters

Your brand is the size of your pie.

Every sale creates a pie of value, and the price you charge is just one slice. A strong brand makes the whole pie bigger and lets you keep a fairer slice. A weak brand shrinks it: you compete on price, you discount to win, and one cheaper rival can take your customers overnight. Strengthening your brand is the highest-leverage thing most businesses ignore, because they have never had a number for it. Now you will.
