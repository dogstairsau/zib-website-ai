Paid search in Brisbane

Cheaper clicks. A wider map to get wrong.

Brisbane clicks cost less than Sydney's or Melbourne's in most categories, and the field is thinner. That is the good news, and it is why a well-run Brisbane account can return more per dollar than the same account in a southern capital. The bad news is that cheaper clicks make waste easier to hide. An account can look healthy on cost per click and still be buying the wrong searches.

The Brisbane-specific problem is geography. South East Queensland is one search market spread across Brisbane, Logan, Ipswich, Moreton Bay, the Redlands, and often the Gold Coast and Sunshine Coast. Most inherited accounts here either target "Brisbane" and miss half the customers, or target all of Queensland and pay for enquiries the business cannot service.

We build location targeting around where you can actually take the job, bid by area on what each one produces, and use the search term data to decide where the Brisbane SEO effort goes next. Paid finds the demand; organic keeps it.

Account takeover vs new build

An inherited Brisbane account carries conversion history worth keeping and structure usually worth replacing. We audit before we touch anything. A new account starts with tracking, then a tight Search build, and earns the budget for more.

Search vs PMax vs Demand Gen

Search captures intent. Performance Max finds volume but will cannibalise brand traffic without exclusions. Demand Gen creates interest that Search later captures. Each is measured against the others, not reported in isolation.

Call tracking and lead attribution

Brisbane trades and services close on the phone. Dynamic call tracking, enhanced conversions and offline import of closed jobs mean the account learns from won work, not from form fills.
