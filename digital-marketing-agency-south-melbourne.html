Local search, done properly

Ranking in your suburb is a different job to ranking in Australia.

A national SEO program and a local one share maybe a third of their tactics. If your customers are within ten kilometres of your front door, the map pack is worth more than page one — and the map pack is won with proximity, prominence and relevance, not blog volume.

That's the work we do for South Melbourne businesses, and it's the same work we did on this page. Read it as a worked example.

Google Business Profile Map pack strategy Local schema Review velocity

  1. Google Business Profile, run like an asset

    Categories, services, products, attributes, Q&A and posts — all of it is ranking surface. Most profiles are filled in once and never touched again, which is exactly why the ones that are maintained win.

  2. Proximity you can't change, prominence you can

    You can't move your office closer to the searcher. You can out-review, out-cite and out-signal every competitor in the radius, which is what actually decides the three-pack between businesses of similar distance.

  3. Suburb pages that say something

    One page per suburb with the town name swapped is a spam signal, not a strategy. Real local pages carry real local detail — which is why this one names streets, postcodes and trams.

  4. Reviews as a ranking input, not a vanity metric

    Volume, recency, velocity and the words inside them. A steady drip of reviews that mention the service and the suburb beats a burst of five-star silence every time.

  5. Structured data doing the translating

    LocalBusiness markup with geo coordinates, opening hours and service area tells a search engine — and an AI answer engine — exactly what and where you are. This page ships with it. Most local pages don't.

Common questions

What South Melbourne businesses actually ask us.

In Melbourne, most agency retainers land between $2,000 and $15,000 a month depending on how many channels you're running and how much media sits on top. Ours is no different — and the honest answer is that we start adding real commercial value at around $5–8k a month across media and retainer combined. Below that you're usually better served running it yourself with our free audit as the roadmap, and we'll tell you that on the first call rather than take the money.

Only if the maths works. An agency is worth it when the cost of the retainer is smaller than the margin on the extra revenue it produces, and when the work needs skills you'd otherwise have to hire three people to cover — search, paid media, creative and analytics. It isn't worth it if you're buying activity you can't tie to a number, which is why we report in leads, sales, ROAS and CAC rather than impressions. If we can't see a path to paying for ourselves, we'll say so.

Five questions worth asking anyone, us included. Who specifically does the work, and are they senior? What happens to my account if that person leaves? What's the notice period, and is there a clawback? Can you show me a client in my category with numbers rather than logos? And what would you tell me not to spend money on? The last one is the tell — an agency that can't name something it wouldn't sell you is selling you everything.

There's no single best one, and any agency claiming the title is telling you something about its marketing rather than its work. The useful question is which agency is best for your business: a national eCommerce brand and a South Melbourne trades business need almost opposite things. Look for relevant category experience, senior people who stay on the account, reporting you can hand to an accountant, and a contract you can leave. We're a Premier Google Partner and Meta Business Partner rating 4.8 from 90+ reviews, and we've been doing this since 2009 — but judge us on the audit, not the badges.

Local search is decided largely by three things: how close you are to the searcher, how prominent your business is (reviews, citations, links, brand signals), and how relevant your profile and site are to what they typed. That makes Google Business Profile, review velocity and structured data as important as your website content. National SEO is a different weighting entirely — more content, more authority, less geography.
