Pricing

Every job is priced on its own. Here's what moves the number.

A ten-panel job on a tight block can be harder work than a hundred-panel job on an open site, so a price list here would only mislead you. These are the six things we look at — tell us about them and you'll have a real number the same day.

01

The run

How many metres, and what that works out to in panels. Each panel covers 2.5m, so a 100m perimeter is 40 panels.

02

The site

Access, ground and how tight the work is. A truck that can back onto an open site is a different job to carting panels down a laneway.

03

Bracing and scrim

Shade cloth and printed mesh turn a fence into a sail, so they bring bracing with them. On a big exposed run that is tonnes of support stays.

04

The crew it takes

Some jobs are one person for forty minutes. A fully braced and scrimmed event run can be a nine-person day.

05

How long you need it

A weekend for an event prices differently to months on a build. Long hires renew rather than needing a fresh quote each time.

06

What else is going out

Barriers, toilets, mats or gates on the same order travel on the same truck, which is cheaper than booking them separately.

04 / Answers

The questions we get asked every week.

If yours isn't here, call and ask — the answer takes about a minute.

How quickly can you get fencing on my site?

It depends on the run, the crew and what's already out on hire. Tell us your start date and we'll give you a straight answer on whether we can hit it. If it's genuinely urgent, call rather than fill in the form — sometimes we can squeeze a job in and sometimes we honestly can't, and you'll know either way in the one conversation. The phone is answered 24/7, including public holidays.

Why isn't there a price list on this page?

Because it would mislead you. A ten-panel job on a tight inner-suburban block can take longer than a hundred-panel job on an open site, and the number moves with access, bracing, scrim, how many crew it takes to stand up and how long you need it. Every job is quoted on its own, and the price is fixed in writing before anything leaves the yard.

Who actually answers the phone?

The owner, most of the time. Ring around and you'll often get someone reading availability off a screen who's never stood a panel up. Here you'll get the person who owns the gear, knows what it will and won't do on your site, and will walk the job through with you before quoting it.

How much fencing do I actually need?

Each panel covers 2.5 metres, so divide your perimeter by 2.5 and round up. If you'd rather not measure it, tell us the address and what you're fencing — we do this every day and can size it from the block.

Do I need a council permit?

If the fence sits wholly on private property, generally no. If it occupies footpath, nature strip or road reserve, most Victorian councils require an occupation permit, and some require a traffic management plan with it. Tell us where the line falls and we'll tell you what your council will want — we can arrange traffic plans and permits as part of the job.

What's the minimum hire period?

Tell us your start date and roughly how long you need it, and we'll price the period you actually want — a weekend for an event, months for a build. Long hires renew rather than needing a fresh quote every time, which is why most of our site work turns into repeat business.

Will it stand up in the wind?

A properly braced run will. Unbraced fencing carrying shade cloth or printed mesh acts like a sail — that's how fences end up flat on a footpath. We fit bracing at set intervals sized to your site's exposure, and we strongly recommend it on any run with cloth or screening on it.

What happens if a panel is damaged on site?

Fair wear and tear is expected and isn't charged back to you. Genuine damage — a panel driven into by an excavator, a stolen run — is charged at replacement value, and we'll tell you what that is when we quote rather than surprising you on the final invoice.

Can you deliver outside business hours?

Yes. Event bump-ins, night works and school-holiday jobs regularly run outside hours, and we schedule around them. We're open 24/7 including public holidays.

Do you work with councils and larger builders?

Regularly. We can quote fencing, barriers, toilets and mats on a single purchase order, invoice against a project number, and hold a scheduled delivery window for a bump-in. Ask for the council and events pricing when you call.
