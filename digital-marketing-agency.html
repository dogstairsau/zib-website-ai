Common questions

What Australian businesses ask us first.

At the useful end, it takes responsibility for a commercial number and then runs whichever channels reach it: search engine optimisation, paid search, paid and organic social, email, content and the website itself. At the less useful end, it sells channel activity and reports on the activity rather than the outcome. The distinction matters more than any list of services, because both kinds of agency describe themselves identically.

Single-channel retainers for small businesses generally start in the low four figures a month. Multi-channel programmes for established businesses commonly run from the mid four figures upward, and enterprise engagements are scoped individually. Ad spend sits on top and is paid directly to the platforms. The more useful question than "what does it cost" is what a customer is worth to you, because that determines what you can afford to pay to acquire one.

In-house wins on product knowledge and responsiveness. Agencies win on specialist depth and breadth, because one salary rarely buys competence in technical SEO, paid media, creative and analytics simultaneously. Many of our clients run both: an internal marketing manager who owns the brand and the brief, with us supplying the specialist execution underneath. That structure usually beats either extreme.

Proximity matters less than it used to, since search and paid media are national channels and most collaboration happens on video calls. What's worth checking locally is whether the agency understands your market and can meet in person when something goes wrong. We're headquartered in Melbourne with growth partners across Sydney, Brisbane, Adelaide, Canberra, the Gold Coast and Geelong, so most Australian businesses have someone reachable nearby.

Paid search and paid social produce data immediately and meaningful performance within 60 to 90 days. SEO typically takes 6 to 12 months to mature on competitive terms, though technical fixes on an established site can move things much faster. Any agency promising transformative organic results in a quarter is either working on an unusually easy market or overselling.

No. We'll be direct that SEO needs a fair runway before it can be judged, but you shouldn't be trapped with an agency that isn't performing. Our retention runs at 97% year on year, which we'd rather earn than enforce.

If you need leads this quarter, paid search. If you're building an asset that keeps producing after the spend stops, SEO. If your product is discovered rather than actively searched for, paid social.
